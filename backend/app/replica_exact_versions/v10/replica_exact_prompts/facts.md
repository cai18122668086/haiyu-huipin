# Resolve the product facts once

Inspect only the original product images, their source-bound readable marks and
every supplied user_statement. Split combined statements into individual facts
where needed, retaining their source IDs. Cover every user_statement, including
claims excluded or uncertain; do not silently discard unrelated supported benefits.

Return approved, excluded or uncertain for each fact with its concrete reason and
user_statement_ids and/or product_marks (product_index, zero-based mark_index).
Direct contradictions about physical identity/ingredients follow clearly readable
current packaging. Explicit user claims remain authorized when merely absent from
photos. An incomplete or unreadable ingredient list does not prove absence. Never
infer a function from an ingredient alone. Use truthful broader facts when needed.
Product marks are fallible observations: correct an OCR interpretation using the
attached pixels and cite the actual readable mark. Do not invent microprint.

These decisions will be shared by all screens, planning, generation and reviews.
Explain exclusions precisely so later stages do not re-litigate the same conflict.
Do not require the user or an operator to choose a version during execution.
