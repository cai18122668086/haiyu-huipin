# Split each actual column into physical commercial screens

Inspect the attached column pixels from top to bottom. A new dominant headline
with a distinct buyer question, hero scene or evidence mechanism begins another
screen even when its gradient, palette or background continues without a divider.
Do not merge a hero, a pain explanation, a demonstration and an ingredient proof
into one long screen. Minor subtitles and labels inside one continuous evidence
carrier do not by themselves create more screens. Do not assume equal heights or
a fixed count per column.

Keep a headline with the proof, action or scene it governs, as required by the
original automatic-segmentation Skill. A title-only strip above its supporting
photo is not a separate sales panel just because the photo starts in a rounded
card or changes background color. A new screen requires its own self-contained
commercial composition; preserve a heading and its dependent demonstration,
ingredient labels, comparisons or chart together.

Apply the same dependency test to a poster's bottom region. A price, coupon,
buy-one-get-one offer, discount, campaign date or sales-count strip that
completes the product/hero above is supporting content of that SAME screen.
A bold offer headline, colored banner or clear gap alone does not make it an
independent composition. Before cutting, verify that the lower region has its
own self-contained hero, demonstration or evidence carrier beyond the offer.
Keep a product hero and its dependent promotional footer together; do not turn
the footer into an output image on its own. This is a composition dependency
rule, not a rule about image aspect ratio or a fixed number of screens.
This exception covers transactional purchase terms only. A lower card presenting
a product property, ingredient, nutrition metric, performance comparison or
benefit with its own illustration, chart or explanatory mechanism is NOT a
promotional footer. Preserve such self-contained evidence cards as separate
screens when they introduce distinct commercial duties. In a stack of rounded
cards, ingredient pictures, a protein/standard comparison chart, and a separate
gut-health mechanism are distinct evidence carriers even under one overall
heading. A shared heading or matching card color does not merge those carriers.

Return every internal horizontal boundary as an increasing y coordinate in the
attached COLUMN image, not the original montage. The server adds 0 and the column
height, supplies full-width bounds, and maps cuts back to original coordinates.
Describe one ordered screen per resulting interval: its visible dominant headline
(or a concrete scene label when it has no headline) and the buyer question it answers.
These observed words are evidence, not approved replacement poster copy.
Place a cut in the clear interval AFTER the preceding panel's final dependent
caption and BEFORE the next panel's headline. Do not cut through a small caption
under an ingredient object or detach a chart's title from the chart it governs.

On a count mismatch, re-open only the requested columns and correct missing or
spurious cuts using actual headline and scene transitions. Other columns remain
frozen. Earlier observations may be wrong; never invent a screen to satisfy them.

Read source-column y coordinates from the numbered gutter when a
reference_column_coordinate_guide is supplied. That gutter is outside the source
pixels, not a commercial panel, divider, headline or chart. Image height and all
source y coordinates are unchanged; do not estimate y from a displayed thumbnail.
coordinate_evidence.blank_bands lists measured bright empty intervals across
the source column. These are advisory geometry only: they neither determine a
screen count nor prove a semantic transition. After deciding which complete
compositions exist, use the matching gap AFTER all dependent content and BEFORE
the next dominant headline. Never choose a line inside an illustration or small
ingredient caption merely because it looks approximately half-way down.
Independent commercial duties can share a topic: a formulation/ingredient
mechanism and a separate headline-led experiment/measurement panel must each
remain complete when both are visibly self-contained. A single outer rounded
card or shared gradient is not evidence that the entire column is one screen.
