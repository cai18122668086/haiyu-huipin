# Visual performance contract

Use this layer beside `visual-proof.md`, never in place of it.

- `visual_proof_contract` answers whether the screen truthfully proves the selling point.
- `module_visual_performance_contract` answers whether that proof becomes a forceful, layered, product-specific sales image.

Do not merge them into a vague request for “丰富、高级、有冲击力”. Every performance instruction must name a visible consequence.

## Duty routing

| Strategy | Required visible force |
|---|---|
| `FIRST_SCREEN_CORE_BENEFIT` | High commercial energy, large product, strong headline/evidence contrast, at least three mobile scan stops, and a clear return to the buying reason. |
| `PAIN` | Magnify the real problem situation through action, contact, consequence or truthful contrast; icons cannot carry the pain alone. |
| `EFFICACY_PROOF` | Make the product or result the main visual and keep the decisive evidence out of small side cards. |
| `MECHANISM` | Show source -> working path -> result when verified; never invent internal anatomy. |
| `MATERIAL_INGREDIENT` | Use whole-product plus verified macro detail, texture, particle, liquid or contact behavior at two scales. |
| `USE_SCENE` | Prioritize person action, product contact and credible environment over static staging. |
| `PARAMETER_SPECIFICATION` | Use the number as an anchor but connect it to scale, action, capacity or a real purchase consequence; avoid a table wall. |
| `TRUST` | Lead with the supported buyer conclusion, then make the readable report, certificate, process or source secondary evidence. |
| `PURCHASE_CLOSURE` | Return to the complete product, core benefit, applicable buyer and final purchase reason. |
| `OTHER_PRODUCT_PROOF` | State a product-specific visible strategy and use the same measurable contract fields; never fall back to generic packshot styling. |

## Runtime-derived per-module contract

For ordinary modules, the runtime derives the following fields from story function, duty, order, breathing-screen status, visible copy and visual proof:

- strategy and target energy;
- minimum product prominence and whether the headline must enter the top three visual ranks;
- one primary evidence anchor and whether it must be the main visual;
- minimum depth layers and a concrete foreground/midground/background plan;
- a visible tension device such as scale contrast, state contrast, action tension or whole/macro dual scale;
- maximum permitted background competition;
- information density, typography-role target, unequal information-group target and mobile scan-stop target;
- explicit scan order and commercial closure;
- creative permissions grounded in the current evidence;
- forbidden regressions.

Default forbidden regressions include product plus one detached headline, decoration replacing evidence, equal-weight information, a generic studio background, and an attractive image that does not reveal what or why it sells. Write `module_visual_performance_contract` only for a genuine exception, include `performance_override_reason`, and keep overrides to the compiler's small minority budget. Repeating the full payload across ordinary screens is a compilation error.

## Creative permission

When supported by verified evidence, actively allow whole/macro dual scale, truthful state contrast, real action/contact moments, real liquid/foam/particle/fiber/material response, source-to-result pathways, product/evidence scale contrast, bounded commercial amplification, unmistakably illustrative metaphors, controlled foreground overlap/perspective/depth, and result scenes instead of icon-only explanation.

These permissions never authorize an invented ingredient, structure, report, measurement, result, accessory or capability.

## v4.3 expression payload

In addition to payload count, record the actual cast: hero subject, supporting subjects, evidence object, action/path and result/context. A normal non-breathing screen needs at least three distinct cast nodes. Ingredient, material, mechanism and process screens must let those subjects occupy the planned first-visual role; a full product cannot be silently promoted back to the dominant subject merely because a generator handles packshots more easily.

Content density is counted as distinct, visible, fact-bound information units. Normal proof screens carry 3–5 units; hook/use/result/closure screens carry 2–4; breathing screens carry 0–2. A repeated slogan, decorative English word or duplicated badge does not count.

## Campaign performance curve

Derive the performance rhythm from ordered module duties before M01 generation. High does not mean every screen shouts; it means the page has deliberate peaks and no uncontrolled decline. A full manual `visual_performance_rhythm` ledger is optional compatibility data, not required planning work.

A typical curve may be:

`core benefit HIGH -> pain MEDIUM_HIGH -> efficacy HIGH -> mechanism MEDIUM_HIGH -> material MEDIUM -> use MEDIUM_HIGH -> parameter/trust MEDIUM -> closure HIGH`

Use the actual buyer story, not this example, to choose the order. Only modules explicitly listed as breathing screens may use low information density or fewer than three scan stops. Lower-page modules may become calmer, but may not regress into a generic packshot, equal-card wall or “商品摆拍＋两行字”.

## Pixel gate

Record actual product prominence percentage, headline rank, evidence-primary status, depth layers, typography roles, unequal information groups, mobile scan stops/order, visual tension, commercial closure and background competition. Compare them against the approved contract and fail deterministically when a required minimum or maximum is missed. Decorative icons do not count as evidence, depth, unequal hierarchy or scan stops.
