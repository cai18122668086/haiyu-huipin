# Category-neutral pain-expression routing

Use this reference for every `PAIN_ARTICULATION` or `PAIN_SOLUTION_COMBINED` module. Pain expression is selected from the structure of the buyer problem, never from a product-category template. Examples from health, beauty, appliances, apparel, food, home, pet, digital or other categories illustrate grammar only and do not authorize a claim.

## Diagnose the pain before choosing the layout

Record:

- `pain_subject_type`: `PERSON`, `PRODUCT_OR_OBJECT`, `ENVIRONMENT`, `TASK_OR_PROCESS`, or `SYSTEM_RELATION`;
- `pain_visibility`: `DIRECTLY_VISIBLE`, `PARTIALLY_VISIBLE`, or `ABSTRACT_BUT_ILLUSTRATABLE`;
- `pain_multiplicity`: one dominant pain, several parallel pains, one pain across contexts, or one escalating chain;
- `pain_relation`: `STATE`, `TRIGGER_TO_STATE`, `STATE_TO_CONSEQUENCE`, `TRIGGER_STATE_CONSEQUENCE`, `CONFLICT`, `ACCUMULATION`, or `CONTROLLED_CONTRAST`;
- `emotional_intensity`: `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, or `HIGH`, proportional to the real purchase friction;
- the affected object/person/environment, visible undesired state or action, consequence, and verified value bridge.

Do not begin from “make a pain page”. Begin from the relation the buyer must perceive.

Before locking the expression form, apply [commercial-aversion-control.md](commercial-aversion-control.md). The pain must be recognizable but commercially tolerable; a more graphic close-up is not automatically stronger proof.

## Universal expression-form library

These forms are category-neutral candidates, not fixed templates:

| Expression form | Best fit | Required visible relationship | Suitable structure families |
|---|---|---|---|
| `IMMERSIVE_MOMENT` | one concrete, immediately recognizable pain | affected subject + undesired state/action + consequence in one credible moment | `FULL_BLEED_HERO`, `ASYMMETRIC_EDITORIAL` |
| `MACRO_HOTSPOT` | one small detail carries the decision | contextual whole + magnified problem hotspot + exact binding | `MACRO_DIAGRAM`, `NESTED_INFO_BOARD` |
| `MULTI_PAIN_ARRAY` | several parallel frictions must be scanned | one lead pain plus unequal secondary pain scenes; not equal decorative icons | `FRAMED_LIFESTYLE_PLUS_CARDS`, `PORTRAIT_STRIP_PLUS_PAIN_ARRAY`, `NESTED_INFO_BOARD` |
| `TRIGGER_STATE_CONSEQUENCE_CHAIN` | the buyer must understand how a normal trigger becomes a meaningful consequence | trigger -> affected state -> practical/social/use consequence | `PROCESS_STRIP_PLUS_RESULT`, `STACKED_BANDS`, `SPLIT_PANEL` |
| `BEHAVIORAL_CONSEQUENCE_PROXY` | the literal problem would be repellent, sensitive or visually weak, while its everyday consequence is recognizable | restrained behavior such as hiding, avoiding, hesitating, interrupting or compensating -> the underlying buyer friction | `ASYMMETRIC_EDITORIAL`, `SPLIT_PANEL`, `FRAMED_LIFESTYLE_PLUS_CARDS` |
| `CONTEXT_SPECTRUM` | the same pain appears across people, tasks, times or environments | distinct contexts mapped to one common friction | `PORTRAIT_STRIP_PLUS_PAIN_ARRAY`, `FRAMED_LIFESTYLE_PLUS_CARDS`, `SELECTION_MATRIX` |
| `OBJECT_ENVIRONMENT_FRICTION` | the pain belongs mainly to an object, space, workflow or interaction rather than a face | obstruction, mismatch, wear, clutter, glare, residue, instability or other truthful friction and its use consequence | `SPLIT_PANEL`, `STACKED_BANDS`, `ASYMMETRIC_EDITORIAL` |
| `CONCEPTUAL_CHARACTER_STORY` | the pain is abstract, sensitive or difficult to photograph directly | recognizable character/IP state + action or consequence; clearly illustrative, not diagnostic | `NESTED_INFO_BOARD`, `CENTRAL_OBJECT_ORBIT`, `STACKED_BANDS` |
| `DOCUMENTARY_SEQUENCE` | repeated moments or accumulation create the pain | two or more time/task moments with directional escalation or recurrence | `STACKED_BANDS`, `PROCESS_STRIP_PLUS_RESULT`, `FRAMED_LIFESTYLE_PLUS_CARDS` |
| `SYMBOLIC_PAIN_MAP` | several signs need rapid low-copy orientation | one real or illustrated affected subject + bound symbols/labels showing where or when the pain appears | `CENTRAL_OBJECT_ORBIT`, `NESTED_INFO_BOARD`, `MACRO_DIAGRAM` |
| `CONTROLLED_STATE_CONTRAST` | two truthful conditions can be compared on a preserved basis | condition A versus B with the same object, scale and comparison basis | `COMPARISON_SPLIT` |
| `ASYMMETRIC_EDITORIAL_COLLAGE` | one dominant pain and several supporting consequences need strong hierarchy | one large decisive visual + unequal supporting scenes or symbols + a clear reading path | `ASYMMETRIC_EDITORIAL`, `FRAMED_LIFESTYLE_PLUS_CARDS` |

`IMMERSIVE_MOMENT` is not the default. Use it only when one scene can truthfully carry the complete pain relation. A different person or background inside the same full-bleed structure is not a new expression form.

## Routing rules

1. One visible pain with a decisive physical detail normally routes to `MACRO_HOTSPOT`, not a generic lifestyle poster.
2. Several parallel pains route to `MULTI_PAIN_ARRAY`, `CONTEXT_SPECTRUM`, or `ASYMMETRIC_EDITORIAL_COLLAGE` according to whether the differences are symptoms, contexts, or hierarchy.
3. A pain with meaningful cause and consequence routes to `TRIGGER_STATE_CONSEQUENCE_CHAIN`; arrows and labels may clarify but may not replace the visible endpoints.
4. When literal depiction is unpleasant or commercially damaging, route to `BEHAVIORAL_CONSEQUENCE_PROXY`: show the ordinary hesitation, avoidance, interruption or compensating action caused by the problem. For example, a person may hold back a smile rather than exposing an extreme tooth-stain macro. Keep the reaction restrained and dignified; do not manufacture humiliation, rejection or panic.
5. Pain centered on space, objects, fitting, handling, workflow or environment routes to `OBJECT_ENVIRONMENT_FRICTION`; do not force a worried face into the screen.
6. An abstract or sensitive pain may route to `CONCEPTUAL_CHARACTER_STORY` or `SYMBOLIC_PAIN_MAP` only when unmistakably illustrative and fact-safe.
7. Repetition over time routes to `DOCUMENTARY_SEQUENCE`; do not imply duration, frequency or progression that is not supported.
8. `CONTROLLED_STATE_CONTRAST` requires a truthful comparison basis. Otherwise downgrade to a single-state scene or qualitative illustration.
9. Use black-and-white documentary treatment, color-coded hotspots, editorial typography, IP illustration or irregular collage as style treatments only when compatible with the campaign DNA. They are not separate evidence classes and must not imitate diagnosis, testing or official data.

## Layout and campaign variation

- Keep palette, photographic finish, typography character and recurring motifs consistent with the series DNA while changing geometry, subject scale, camera and information relationship.
- A normal pain module should use at least two meaning-bearing zones unless `IMMERSIVE_MOMENT` is specifically justified by one decisive scene.
- Do not repeat the preceding screen's full-bleed geometry. When the pain screen follows a full-bleed hero, prefer a split, nested, stacked, macro, array or asymmetric structure.
- Mini-scenes must show different actions, states, contexts or consequences. Crops of the same scene do not count.
- Use unequal hierarchy: one lead pain receives the strongest scale; supporting pains orient or expand it. Equal card walls are a fallback, not a default.
- The product may be absent on a dedicated pain screen. A combined pain-solution screen must visibly bridge to verified product value, but the packshot must not erase the pain evidence.

## Copy and truth boundary

- The picture carries the physical state, friction, sequence or consequence. Copy names, qualifies or disambiguates it.
- Short scene captions, symptom names or context labels may accompany visible subjects. A title, icon row, word cloud or alarm symbol alone is not pain evidence.
- Inferred pain may establish safe context but may not become a medical, safety, performance or efficacy claim.
- Do not manufacture lesions, diagnoses, injuries, damage, contamination, failure, fear, social rejection or exaggerated before/after states beyond the supplied evidence and safe ordinary context.
- Do not let an extreme macro, wet bodily texture, severe discoloration, dense pests, waste, decay or contamination dominate the screen. Use the approved comfort strategy and replacement expression when aversion risk is medium or high.
- Reference images may contribute composition, subject scale, collage grammar, annotation style, documentary treatment or IP/story logic. Never copy their category facts, symptoms, people, brand, wording, numbers, proprietary character or layout skeleton.

## Compact module record

For a pain module, add inside `expression`:

```json
{
  "pain_expression": {
    "pain_subject_type": "PRODUCT_OR_OBJECT",
    "pain_visibility": "DIRECTLY_VISIBLE",
    "pain_multiplicity": "SEVERAL_PARALLEL",
    "pain_relation": "STATE_TO_CONSEQUENCE",
    "expression_form": "MULTI_PAIN_ARRAY",
    "emotional_intensity": "MEDIUM",
    "selection_reason": "多个可见问题共同影响同一购买判断，需要一个主问题和不等权小场景快速扫描"
  }
}
```

The record is machine-side and must never appear as pixel text.

## Failure and downgrade

Reject or re-plan before generation when:

- the selected form is justified only by product category or by a reference image;
- a multi-pain plan collapses to one generic full-bleed photograph;
- icons, labels or a worried face carry meaning without a visible affected state or consequence;
- every pain receives equal card weight and no lead problem is readable;
- a trigger, state or consequence endpoint exists only in copy;
- the screen medicalizes, dramatizes or invents unsupported harm;
- the same pain could be sold unchanged for any same-category product without a verified value bridge in the adjacent solution story.

Downgrade to a truthful single-state scene, object/environment friction, clearly conceptual illustration or concise context when evidence cannot support the stronger route.
