# Storyboard matrix

## Purpose

Approve the whole campaign's expression system before generating any pair. Content order alone is not enough; v4.1 presents the atomic screen plans first, then nests adjacent pairs under one approval packet.

For every module record:

- duty and buyer question;
- lead conclusion and supporting facts;
- proof device and evidence class;
- visible content and forbidden inventions;
- composition family;
- camera scale;
- primary text mode and placement logic;
- reading path;
- copy density and visual-payload density as separate fields;
- visual proof contract: buyer takeaway, visual verb, primary/supporting subjects, required proof relations, specificity anchors, hidden-copy test and minimum effective visual payloads;
- runtime-resolved visual-performance contract: duty strategy, product prominence, headline rank, primary evidence anchor, depth, tension, background competition, unequal hierarchy, mobile scan stops, creative permissions and commercial closure; include a manual override only for a documented exception;
- module spatial-structure contract: observable structure family, zone geometry and weights, structural relationship, full-bleed permission, panel/mini-scene counts, neighbor difference, series-master layout prohibition and anti-collapse fallbacks;
- proof-relation strength and commercial energy;
- exact visible-copy whitelist;
- brand-anchor treatment;
- inherited visual-DNA tokens;
- deliberate variation from the previous module.

At campaign level, record `selling_point_depth_contract` before the storyboard matrix. Every must-remember claim needs an evidence ceiling, required decision jobs, scheduled modules and at least one dedicated or visually dominant module. An umbrella overview does not automatically satisfy the depth of each independent primary sub-point.

The runtime derives visual-performance, visual-carrier, typography and spatial-structure rhythm directly from ordered compact modules. Do not duplicate them in campaign ledgers. Only named breathing screens may reduce density or scan stops. Do not plan two consecutive full-bleed single scenes. Eight screens normally use four or five observable structure families; six or seven use at least three; shorter multi-screen pages use at least two. Every rolling three-screen window needs at least two structural relationships.

## Composition families

Use the smallest family vocabulary that fits the campaign:

- `hero_anchor`;
- `immersive_scene`;
- `asymmetric_editorial`;
- `macro_material`;
- `comparison_split`;
- `process_sequence`;
- `structure_diagram`;
- `data_evidence`;
- `origin_story`;
- `sku_selection`;
- `specification_closure`.
- `directed_pathway`;
- `interaction_state`;
- `installation_fit`;
- `selection_matrix`.

These composition families remain high-level planning labels. They do not replace the observable `module_spatial_structure_contract`; compile each family into visible zones and relationships.

## Scheduling rules

- Do not repeat both the same composition family and the same primary text mode in adjacent modules unless a written transition reason makes the repetition purposeful.
- Alternate camera scale, copy density, visual-payload density and proof form across the page.
- Preserve at least two visible visual-DNA tokens in every module.
- A rational high-copy-density module should normally receive a lower-copy-density sensory, scene or result neighbor, while that neighbor still carries enough visual payload to communicate without its copy.
- Reserve high-impact headline-led structures for moments that deserve a stop; do not ban them.
- Page transitions must preserve the buyer's question flow, not merely visual alternation.
- For advanced archetypes, record the relation endpoints and downgrade route; for multi-entity pages, record hero/companion/accessory/variant/context-only roles.
- Audit every rolling three-module window across duty, visual carrier, camera scale, and primary text mode. Repetition on three or four axes requires an explicit story reason or a planned variation.
- Audit actual spatial geometry separately. A new background or subject inside the same full-bleed poster does not satisfy structural variation.

## User-facing approval

Show the user a concise matrix with:

`模块 | 页面职责 | 主结论 | 画面如何证明 | 商品出镜策略 | 空间结构 | 文字如何进入画面 | 与前后屏关系`

The compiler writes this matrix and the preceding operation/count/3:8-pair decision tables to `campaign-approval.md`, followed by the hierarchical group→screen packet. Show that file verbatim. `空间结构` is a mandatory human-readable summary of the resolved structure family and meaning-bearing zones; it may not be omitted or replaced by the legacy generic `构图与景别` column.

Also show one campaign visual-DNA paragraph. Approval authorizes the campaign logic and expression matrix, not fixed coordinates.
