# Reference intelligence

## Corpus basis

The initial knowledge base was distilled from 65 supplied reference boards, grouped into 21 product sets and decomposed into 193 semantic modules. A later domestic-commerce expansion audited 100 additional boards, grouped them into 28 product sets plus one design-system attachment, split 254 identifiable detail-page columns, and deduplicated them into 294 canonical modules. The combined evidence spans food, pet, beauty, personal care, apparel, gaming devices, household cleaning, laundry, kitchen appliances, bathroom systems, sleep products and home accessories.

The source corpus is an analysis set, not a reusable asset library. Do not copy source pixels into commercial output.

## Repeated high-value patterns

1. Strong first screens combine product identity, one large benefit, and one unmistakable visual anchor.
2. Food and pet-food pages make texture, ingredients, pouring, cutting, spooning and eating behavior carry sensory proof.
3. Beauty and supplement pages alternate aspiration with mechanism, test evidence and usage context.
4. Durable-goods pages convert parts, materials, opening, loading, pressure, cleaning and comparison into physical proof.
5. Trust modules perform best when certificates or reports remain secondary to a clear buyer conclusion.
6. Parameter panels become persuasive only after a scale, scenario, comparison or outcome explains why the number matters.
7. Effective long pages vary density and camera while preserving color roles, typography family and finish.
8. Weak pages repeat slogans, overuse equal cards, shrink evidence, or let lower modules become specification dumps.
9. Complex durable and personal-care products often need directed pathway, trigger–feedback–result, installation-fit, or selection-matrix pages instead of generic mechanism/parameter cards.
10. Multi-device sets and body-contact products require explicit entity roles, physical orientation and action evidence before aesthetic styling.
11. Strong selling-point visualization binds the conclusion to a visible carrier: contact depth, movement direction, state transition, scale relation, same-condition contrast, readable document, or spatial fit.

## Reference identity isolation

Classify every supplied reference before extracting grammar:

- `PRODUCT_EVIDENCE`: current-product facts and identity;
- `VISUAL_GRAMMAR_REFERENCE`: page-duty, proof relation, composition, camera, text and rhythm inspiration only;
- `DESIGN_SYSTEM_ATTACHMENT`: typography, color, spacing, graphic language or finish tokens only;
- `CONTEXT_REFERENCE`: mood or environment only.

A design-system attachment must never enter claim coverage, selling-point ranking, product identity, module count, or proof selection. It may influence the approved visual passport only. A mood/context reference cannot authorize a product state, accessory, person, result, number, certificate, or internal structure.

## Mandatory decomposition and planning bridge

A supplied board may contain one page, several page crops, or a complete multi-column detail-page presentation. Never treat the outer image file as one indivisible reference.

1. Split every identifiable page or semantic panel into an `analyzed_unit` with a stable unit ID and source region.
2. For each unit record its page duty, visual carrier, composition logic, typography logic, complete-product presence (`DOMINANT`, `SUPPORTING`, `IDENTITY_TOKEN_ONLY`, or `ABSENT`), the actual primary subject, how product attribution survives when the product is absent, and one concrete observed expression method.
3. Derive the parent reference's `observed_expression_methods` exactly from those units. A generic style label such as “高级”“清爽” or “电商感” is not analysis.
4. When a method is useful, create an adopted pattern that cites both the reference ID and the exact unit IDs, explains how it will be rebuilt for the current product, records any transferable product-presence rhythm, and schedules the modules that will use it.
5. When a reference is unsuitable, explicitly reject it with a reason. Silent ignoring is invalid.
6. A reference may influence visual grammar only. It cannot add a product fact, resolve a product conflict, dictate the screen count, or override the approved plan.

The operational chain is therefore:

`reference file -> individual analyzed units -> observed method -> adapted pattern -> scheduled module -> pixel audit observation`

## v4.3 adoption record

Every adopted pattern additionally records `fact_dependencies` and `copy_or_claims_not_inherited`. `fact_dependencies` must resolve to active verified facts, and `scheduled_module_ids` must not be empty. `copy_or_claims_not_inherited` explicitly names source-brand wording, numbers, certifications, conclusions or other claims that the current campaign is forbidden to inherit. A pattern with no fact dependency or no scheduled screen is analysis only and cannot enter a generation prompt.

If any link is missing, the reference has not actually been used.

## Style families observed

- high-saturation playful commercial: bold type, mascots, ingredient cutouts, strong blocks;
- clean scientific rational: pale field, molecule/structure macro, charts and restrained accent colors;
- premium dark durable: metal, hard rim light, shields, exploded structure and large numeric hierarchy;
- soft natural lifestyle: botanical light, tactile still life, low-contrast typography and material close-up;
- youthful editorial beauty: model-led collage, expressive type, product swatches and social-media energy;
- warm food narrative: cream/brown/orange palette, pouring texture, origin and process story.

Choose a family only when it follows from the current product and buyer. Mix grammars deliberately; never paste a category preset.

## Anti-copy rule

Extract abstract relationships such as “macro texture proves softness” or “operation sequence proves convenience.” Do not extract exact copy, mascot, celebrity/model identity, composition coordinates, branded colors, certificate art, or a complete page skeleton.
