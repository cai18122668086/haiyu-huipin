# Core policy

## Non-negotiable hierarchy

Preserve in this order:

1. Product identity.
2. Factual and legal compliance.
3. Module role and buyer conclusion.
4. Visual proof of the lead value.
5. Commercial impact and desire.
6. Legibility.
7. Campaign continuity.
8. Decoration.

Never trade a higher item for a lower one.

## v4.1 user checkpoints

Ask the user to intervene only at:

1. Approval of the complete hierarchical group→screen plan (all atomic screens and ordered pairs together).
2. Final review of the delivered single-screen files and group audits. A long-page assembly is an optional preview, not a gate.

Between these checkpoints, the Agent owns ordinary inspection, repair decisions, continuity calibration, and progression. Stop earlier only when a missing fact would make the work misleading or a new direction invalidates the approved plan. Exhausting the one-repair budget does not pause the campaign: close the second attempt with residuals and continue.

When the run is dispatched by an unattended queue and the submission itself carries the user's authorization (the run input states that submitting the job equals approving the plan), treat both checkpoints as pre-approved, proceed through generation and delivery, and record that assumption in the delivery note. Queue-side authorization never overrides a factual, identity or compliance stop condition: those still stop the run and are reported as missing facts rather than filled in.

## Reference safety

- Reference pages teach composition, rhythm, proof form, density, color roles, and typography hierarchy.
- They do not authorize copying a product, brand, copy line, number, certificate, proprietary mascot, model likeness, or layout skeleton.
- Do not preserve screenshots' black borders, app chrome, watermarks, creator IDs, or capture artifacts.

## Current-product ownership

The current product must remain the visual anchor. Any person, hand, scene, icon, diagram, ingredient, material, or result exists to clarify this product's verified value. Remove unrelated decorative evidence.

## Stop conditions

Block planning or generation when:

- no usable current-product identity evidence exists;
- no readable authentic brand mark exists in the user-supplied current-product evidence **and** the user has not confirmed the product as white-label. When the user explicitly confirms that the product carries no brand mark, lock `brand_logo_source.mode = BRANDLESS` with `brandless_evidence` and continue: every screen then carries zero independent logos and the first screen is identified by the product subject, truthful category/specification lettering and visual DNA instead of a logo. Never invent, approximate or imitate a mark to satisfy the branded gate;
- required numerical or regulatory claims have no source;
- conflicting facts cannot be resolved without user choice;
- the requested result depends on imitating a protected brand asset or a real person's likeness without authorization;
- the target platform profile is claimed as exact but cannot be verified.
