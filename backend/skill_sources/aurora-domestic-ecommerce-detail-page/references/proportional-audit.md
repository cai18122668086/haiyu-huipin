# Proportional visual audit (v4.1)

For v4.1 plans, audit information sufficiency separately from visual polish: confirm the unique M01 hero, concrete buyer-question progression, Pair information delta, purchase judgment, and absence of posterization regression.

For v4.1 modules, semantic visual audit is independent from payload counting. A product, three icons and a headline may satisfy a naive element count while proving nothing. Inspect whether the planned carrier is visible, whether the core meaning survives hidden copy, whether pain and consequence are concrete, whether the product-solution bridge is visible, and whether another same-category packshot could replace the product without changing the screen. The optional long-page audit names the pain modules and solution modules rather than passing continuity by assertion.

## Module audit order

Judge actual pixels in this order:

1. Output format: the recorded raster is exactly the approved width and height. Near-ratio sources may be minimally center-cropped and resized proportionally. Larger mismatches first receive a bounded, symmetric crop and only the remaining mismatch uses edge extension, so later modules do not collapse into a narrow inner poster. Non-uniform stretching is forbidden.
2. Identity: same product, variant, packaging, parts, count and physical relationships.
3. Facts: visible copy, numbers, conditions, certification and comparison.
4. Duty: intended buyer question and lead conclusion are recognizable.
5. Proof: current-product evidence visibly carries the lead claim.
6. Visual performance contract: compare product prominence, headline rank, evidence-primary status, depth layers, unequal information groups, typography roles, visual tension, mobile scan stops, background competition and commercial closure against the approved module thresholds.
7. Proof integrity: visible contents, texture, ingredients, results, reports and comparisons match the declared evidence class.

## v4.3 long-page expression audit

After per-screen judgments, recompute from observed pixels—not the plan—the primary-subject class, presentation form, camera scale, complete-product role and expression signature of every screen. The long page fails when actual subject/form diversity falls below the approved contract, dominant complete-product screens exceed the 8/10/12-screen budget of 3/3/4, or adjacent screens collapse to the same observed signature. Record the actual screen IDs behind every count so a passing summary cannot hide repeated bottle posters.

Each screen also records which planned content units are actually visible. Missing fact-bound units and an unplanned complete product on an absence/token screen are material contract failures even when the overall image is polished.
8. Legibility: Chinese text, numeric hierarchy, contrast and safe area.
9. Text integration: placement follows the subject, action, evidence or negative space and does not default to an unjustified poster title.
10. Series continuity and logo scope: visual-DNA tokens remain in the same campaign; in a `BRANDED` campaign M01 shows exactly one independent logo that visibly matches the authorized source, in a `BRANDLESS` campaign M01 shows none and is anchored by the product subject plus visual DNA, every later screen shows none in both modes, and package-printed logos remain natural product identity without counting toward the independent-logo total.
11. Page distinctness: composition, camera, text mode, reading path, copy density and visual-payload density differ purposefully from adjacent modules.
12. Hidden-copy comprehension: after covering all copy, the buyer can still identify the product-specific claim from subjects, actions and relations.
13. Pixel hygiene: no module IDs, page numbers, workflow labels, next-screen hints, claim IDs or planning vocabulary appear in the rendered image.
14. Integrity: no impossible geometry, broken hands/parts, source leakage or third-party marks.

Record concrete observed evidence for every result. Never audit from a prompt or filename.

## Material failures

Permit automatic repair only for:

- product identity or factual error;
- unsupported claim or fabricated proof;
- unreadable or corrupted essential Chinese;
- missing visual product evidence for a renderable lead claim;
- any deterministic module visual-performance threshold missed, including undersized product, demoted evidence, flat depth, equal-weight information, missing scan stops, excessive background competition or absent commercial closure;
- module duty no longer recognizable;
- impossible geometry or broken product relation;
- obvious source-background or third-party asset leakage;
- severe campaign discontinuity;
- any independent, floating, corner, header, footer, watermark or repeated logo on M02 onward, or on any screen of a `BRANDLESS` campaign;
- fabricated photorealistic product proof;
- essential text placed as an unrelated overlay that breaks the approved visual proof;
- mechanical repetition of both composition family and text mode when it materially collapses the approved long-page rhythm;
- critical seam content cut or any width/height mismatch;
- missing, duplicated or source-mismatched independent brand logo on M01.

An unapproved OCR phrase is material only when it changes a product fact, efficacy, quantity, condition, safety/trust claim, or becomes a dominant sales conclusion. A short scene caption in a pain module may be recorded as advisory when it merely names a visibly obvious situation, remains bound to that situation, and does not strengthen the product claim. Numeric indices such as `1/2/3` or `01/02/03` are not product numbers when they visibly organize an existing action sequence or panel order. Record them as `advisory_visual_marks`; do not spend the repair budget merely to remove them.

A generic bacteria, fungus, mite, odor or stain symbol is material only when it presents itself as microscopy, laboratory evidence, a named organism, a diagnostic depiction, or an unsupported product mechanism/result. The same proportional rule applies to unlabeled molecule-like spheres, links and node networks: when they are clearly stylized visual rhetoric for a verified formula or ingredient-family idea, they need not reproduce an exact molecule and are not a repair reason merely because their shape was not specified in the plan. They become material only when labels, bonds, scientific framing or surrounding copy make them look like a verified exact structure, mechanism, test or result.

## Advisory issues

Do not spend the repair budget on:

- multiple related selling points;
- useful information bars;
- a stronger composition than the plan sketch;
- slight spacing, color or decoration preference;
- omitted optional information;
- non-identical but compatible layout families.
- short non-claim scene captions that only clarify an already visible pain situation;
- harmless variation in a clearly conceptual bacteria, fungus, mite, odor or stain symbol;
- minor illustrative-shape differences that do not imply microscopy, diagnosis, testing or a stronger efficacy claim.
- consecutive step indices, arrows, bullets and separators that organize already visible actions without adding a claim;
- unlabeled decorative molecule-like or node-network graphics that remain clearly conceptual and subordinate;
- any proposed repair whose only effect is removing one of the above while weakening hierarchy, scan order or visual tension.

When the first render is factually safe and the questioned element falls entirely inside this advisory list, keep the first render. A repair must remove a material buyer-risk or restore an approved core relation; visual simplification by itself is not an improvement.

## Long-page audit

In addition to visual continuity, audit commercial memory against `campaign_recall_contract`:

- the first screen communicates a product-specific value reason rather than identity alone;
- the selected pain or buyer doubt is answered by an evidence-backed product benefit;
- every must-remember claim receives visible proof or a truthful qualification;
- the closing module reinforces at least one priority buying reason rather than ending with generic product display;
- after viewing the page, the top buying reasons can be stated without inventing unsupported claims.

Inspect the assembled page for:

- first-screen stopping power;
- stable product identity end to end;
- real proof of core claims;
- purchase-decision order and natural transitions;
- repeated claims or proof forms;
- independent high/low copy-density and visual-payload rhythm;
- alternation among scene, material, mechanism, parameter and trust;
- one coherent visual world;
- typography-role and placement rhythm;
- brand-anchor continuity through visual DNA, with exactly one source-faithful independent logo on M01 and none on M02 onward;
- truthful proof with no invented product states;
- quality retention in lower modules;
- fidelity to the approved visual-performance curve, including visible peaks and no uncontrolled lower-page decline;
- final purchase closure;
- invisible or intentional seams.

The long-page audit records residual issues. It does not launch another multi-module repair loop.
