# Visual expression routing

## Core rule

Do not begin a module by writing a headline and decorating around it. Begin with what the buyer must perceive, then build the strongest truthful visual relation.

Use:

`claim -> buyer meaning -> evidence class -> visual verb -> subject relation -> composition -> minimum necessary copy`

## v4.3 expression signature

Each screen plans four independent dimensions: `primary_subject_class + presentation_form + camera_scale + complete_product_role`. Their concatenation is the `expression_signature`; adjacent signatures must differ. Subject classes cover complete product, product detail, human/body, ingredient/material, mechanism, action, result, documentary/specification evidence and environment. Presentation forms cover hero poster, multi-pain array, whole-plus-macro, exploded structure, process sequence, source-action-result, state contrast, use-scope matrix, documentary evidence, specification board, FAQ board and asymmetric editorial layout.

Changing only a headline, color, crop or decorative motif is not a new expression. For 8/10/12-screen campaigns, plan at least 4/5/6 primary-subject classes and 5/6/7 presentation forms. Non-breathing screens need a visual cast of at least three distinct nodes so the image has a subject, evidence relation and result/context rather than one bottle plus copy.

## Visual verbs

Route content to a visual action whenever evidence permits:

| Meaning | Useful visual verbs |
|---|---|
| texture or sensory quality | pour, spoon, stretch, cut, break, coat, absorb, melt, reveal a macro surface |
| convenience | open, pull, press, fold, carry, store, clean, compare hand scale, show before/after operation states |
| material or ingredient | isolate, layer, trace, magnify, group, map source, connect ingredient to product |
| structure or durability | explode, section, load, bend, lock, seal, heat, pressure-test, compare verified structures |
| fit or scenario | place the current product in a credible task, body, home, outdoor or pet relationship |
| range or SKU | arrange variants by a clear selection logic, not as an unexplained lineup |
| parameter | connect the number to scale, duration, capacity, anatomy, action or purchase consequence |
| trust | lead with the buyer conclusion; use reports, process or certificates as secondary evidence |
| flow or mechanism | trace a verified source/entry through working nodes to contact zone and visible result |
| control or automation | connect trigger, device feedback, state change and resulting value |
| installation or fit | relate space, orientation, dimensions/clearance, connection and usable final state |
| selection | map one buyer condition or task to one differentiating attribute and recommended option |

Icons, arrows, glows and slogans may clarify a relationship. They do not count as proof by themselves.

## Evidence and invention gate

Classify every planned visible object or state:

1. `DIRECT_VERIFIED`: visible in current-product photography or supplied documentation; may be shown as product proof.
2. `CONDITIONALLY_VERIFIED`: supplied by the user with conditions; may be shown with the same qualification.
3. `SAFE_CONTEXT`: a normal lifestyle, hand or environment relation that does not imply an unverified product state or measured result.
4. `ILLUSTRATIVE_METAPHOR`: an unmistakable diagram or metaphor; label it as illustration and never present it as a test, ingredient amount or actual internal structure.
5. `FORBIDDEN_INVENTION`: an unseen product interior, texture, ingredient, result, certificate, test setup, accessory, quantity or physical behavior; do not render it.

Visual-first never authorizes fabricated evidence. When direct proof is unavailable, choose safe context, a clearly illustrative diagram, sealed-product evidence or concise factual copy.

## Page-role routing

- Hook screens may use symbolic scale and a strong value image.
- Sensory screens should let material behavior carry the message.
- Mechanism screens should reveal a real relationship, not a decorative molecule field.
- Scene screens should make product use unmistakable and keep the current product dominant.
- Data screens should turn a number into purchase meaning.
- Pathway screens should make origin, direction, intermediate nodes and result readable; decorative arrows are insufficient.
- Interaction screens should keep trigger, feedback and result in one chain instead of isolating a UI screenshot.
- Installation screens should prove fit and access in the real spatial envelope before showing a parameter table.
- Selection screens should organize options by buyer condition, not by an unexplained lineup.
- Trust screens should keep documents readable enough to be credible but subordinate to the conclusion.
- Closure screens should clarify variant, package, compatibility, service or final buying reason.

## Failure conditions

Fail the visual concept before generation when:

- the same conclusion would survive unchanged if the current product were removed;
- the image is only a background for a slogan;
- the planned proof depends on an unverified visible product state;
- decorative icons carry more meaning than the product, action or evidence;
- several unrelated selling points have no common visual relationship.

For pathway, interaction, installation, or selection duties, also use `advanced-page-archetypes.md`. For comparisons, measured results, human-body claims, time sequences, or documentary proof, apply `high-risk-proof-conditions.md` before choosing the visual form.
