For unfinished work:

2. enrich every verified fact with `claim_kind`, `display_intent`, `user_prominence` and `named_elements`;
3. rebuild the compact plan with `expression_contract_version=4.3`;
4. evaluate every formula/ingredient/structure fact in `formula_split_contract.evaluated_claims`;
5. add the campaign diversity budget and every module's subject, presentation, product-role, visual-cast, expression-signature and content-unit contracts;

Previously accepted images may be used only as style references after their identity and audit records are rechecked. They are not automatically accepted v4.3 outputs.
