# Continuity and rhythm

v4.1 additionally requires each Pair to carry a declared information delta. Shared palette, light, material or seam continuity cannot substitute for a new buyer question or purchase judgment.

Use this file together with `series-visual-dna.md`, `storyboard-matrix.md`, `adaptive-typography.md` and `continuity-and-variation-audit.md`.

## Mandatory paired rendering (v4.1)

Plan every campaign as 8, 10, or 12 atomic screens, then render them as ordered pairs `(M01,M02)`, `(M03,M04)`, and so on. Each pair is one continuous 3:8 raster canvas; each half remains an independently approved 3:4 screen after fixed-height slicing. The internal seam belongs to a declared quiet transition zone, while each pair-to-pair seam uses the campaign transition token. Pairing changes the raster container only—it never merges screen duties, claims, copy whitelists, or spatial contracts.

For v4.1, the seam is audited against the declared method rather than a generic style statement. The render pipeline is explicitly two-stage: compile each atomic screen contract, then merge the ordered pair into one `PAIR_PLAN_MERGE` prompt (`pair_merge_type=PAIR_PLAN_MERGE`) with a single 3:8 output lock. The image backend receives only that merged prompt; the atomic prompts are retained for window bounds, fingerprints and audits. Every pair records concrete source/target regions, the visible carrier, seam geometry, hard-cut absence, and the method-specific continuity flag. Every independent pair boundary records its approved transition token and visible shared carrier. Missing or generic seam evidence is a failed audit condition.

## Campaign constitution

Freeze campaign-wide roles after plan approval:

- product identity;
- primary, secondary, accent and neutral color roles;
- headline, subhead, body and numeric typography character;
- graphic language and information-surface material;
- photography finish, lighting family and color temperature;
- brand/product tone;
- product/background separation method;
- seam strategy and safe-area policy.

## Required variation

Vary across modules:

- wide, medium, close and macro views;
- complete product, partial proof, person, hand, still life and structure;
- light/dark center, background depth and scene;
- copy density and visual-payload density, treated independently;
- left/right/center reading path;
- rational, sensory and emotional emphasis.

Consistency does not mean identical background, camera or card template.

Variation does not mean changing campaign palette, typography character, lighting finish or brand behavior. Change page grammar while preserving the visual passport.

Before generation, schedule composition family, camera scale, text mode, reading path, copy density, visual-payload density and proof device for the entire sequence. Do not improvise these independently one screen at a time. Low copy never authorizes an empty or weakly informative picture.

## Vertical rhythm

Aim for visible alternation, for example:

`strong hook -> direct difference -> mechanism -> breathing scene -> macro proof -> parameter/trust -> result scene -> purchase closure`

Use the example only as a rhythm model. Reorder according to the actual buyer decision.

## Prompt rehydration

Compile every module prompt from the same session. Include only current product locks, campaign world, current duty, selected facts, proof direction, adjacency, creative freedom, hard boundaries and output surface.

Never use the user's newest sentence as a standalone prompt. Convert it to a delta and preserve all unaffected campaign state.

## Repair continuity

An automatic repair must edit attempt one, preserve passed layers, change only diagnosed failures, and retain the campaign constitution. Original product images remain identity references, not repair canvases.
