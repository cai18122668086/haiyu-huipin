# Adaptive typography

## Principle

Text is part of the scene, evidence and reading path. It is not a fixed title layer placed above every image.

Centered display headlines remain valid for a hook, chapter turn, symmetrical composition or intentional breathing screen. They are one mode, not the default mode.

## Text modes

Choose one primary text mode per module and at most one supporting mode:

- `centered_hero_headline`: centered or near-centered display statement for a major hook or turn;
- `offset_editorial_headline`: headline aligned to meaningful negative space or an asymmetric subject;
- `object_label`: short copy attached to a product, ingredient, component, body area or SKU;
- `directional_annotation`: copy following a gaze, gesture, flow, opening path or process;
- `comparison_caption`: copy bound to left/right, before/after or option structures;
- `step_sequence`: numbers and verbs integrated with an operation path;
- `data_hierarchy`: a number or parameter is primary and its meaning is secondary;
- `scene_caption`: restrained copy placed inside credible scene space;
- `body_explainer`: short paragraph paired with process, origin, ingredient, specification or trust evidence;
- `minimal_label`: almost textless visual with only brand, qualifier and one short conclusion;
- `closing_summary`: compact specification, service, selection or purchase closure.

## Placement decision

Derive placement from:

1. subject and product position;
2. gaze, gesture, motion or material-flow direction;
3. natural negative space;
4. local contrast and background complexity;
5. the intended scan path;
6. whether copy refers to the whole frame or a local object;
7. the previous module's text mode and placement.

Examples:

- place copy in the space a person or animal looks toward;
- align an annotation with a spoon, stream, opening path or structural edge;
- bind comparison labels to the compared objects;
- let a large number occupy the visual scale that its importance deserves;
- place a paragraph beside its evidence rather than floating it over an unrelated hero image.

## Binding per-screen contract

Typography may not be chosen after the image is rendered. Before approval, every module must declare:

- `primary_mode` and optional `supporting_mode`;
- `anchor_target`: the exact product, component, ingredient, action, person, result, comparison, path, or evidence object the copy explains;
- `anchor_zone`: the intended spatial zone, not a generic fallback;
- `reading_direction`: the intended scan path through image and copy;
- `placement_logic`: why this position belongs to the pictured relation;
- `hierarchy` and `max_lines`;
- `container_style`;
- `negative_space_reservation` made before subject placement;
- `image_text_relationship`;
- `composition_flexibility`: `LOCKED`, `NEGATIVE_SPACE_ONLY`, or `CONTROLLED_REFLOW`;
- `neighbor_difference`: the visible typography difference from the previous or next screen.

At campaign level, schedule the primary-mode and anchor-zone sequences before generating M01. Centered hero titles are limited exceptions, and repeated adjacent anchor zones require an explicit story reason. If a collage reference contains several pages, extract typography logic from each page unit rather than treating the collage as one layout.

`CONTROLLED_REFLOW` authorizes local movement or recropping of supporting subjects when the approved text has no credible space. It does not authorize changing product identity, proof relation, facts, page duty, or campaign style. A typography repair that locks the old composition despite missing negative space is invalid.

## Hierarchy and density

- One module has one lead reading conclusion.
- Supporting copy must clarify proof, condition or selection; it must not compete with the lead.
- Prefer short Chinese phrases, meaningful numeric hierarchy and image-led comprehension.
- Long paragraphs require a lower-noise information surface and a reason to exist.
- Do not turn every fact into a badge. Combine related facts into one visual relationship.
- Avoid repeated top-center headline, centered product and symmetrical badge arrangements in adjacent modules.
- Default detail-page copy means 3–5 concise information units on ordinary explanatory screens: one lead conclusion, one short explanatory sentence, one or two bound evidence/parameter/use labels, and only when needed one truth-changing condition or selection qualifier.
- Hook, emotional scene, result and closing screens may use 2–4 units when the image already carries the main meaning. An approved breathing screen may use 0–2.
- Information units are hierarchy roles, not mandatory cards. Combine related copy into one surface or attach labels directly to their objects; do not build an equal-weight badge wall.
- The explanation layer must answer at least one question the headline alone cannot: why the feature matters, how it is used, where it applies, what it connects to, or which condition changes the buying decision.
- The image must still prove; copy must identify, explain, qualify, compare, sequence, or conclude. Repeated slogans and paraphrases do not count as added information.

## Typography continuity

Placement and scale may vary, but the campaign's type character, weight logic, numeric style, color roles and graphic treatment must follow the visual passport.

Variation means changing how type participates in the picture, not changing to a different brand's typography.

## Essential-copy gate

Before generation, classify text as:

- essential conclusion;
- factual qualifier;
- object or process label;
- optional decoration.

Remove optional decoration first. Never remove a condition that changes the truth of a claim.
