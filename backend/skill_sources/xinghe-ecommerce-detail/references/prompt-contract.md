# Prompt Contract

Each detail-page image must be generated independently. Do not place multiple screens into one image.

## Required Prompt Blocks

Every image prompt must include:

1. `vertical 3:4 e-commerce detail page image`
2. `Campaign Style Lock`: unified palette, typography feel, background system, lighting, icon style, whitespace, and product scale.
3. `Design Strength Lock`: category visual motif, page rhythm, composition contrast, signature visual devices, color contrast, and template bans.
4. `Product Fidelity Lock` / `Product Consistency Lock`: keep product appearance, packaging, material, proportions, key pattern, logo/nameplate placement, and structural-part relationships consistent.
5. Current page role: cover, pain point, scene, ingredient, craft, detail, specification, comparison, FAQ, closing, or similar.
6. Current page main title: a short title in the buyer's language, shown inside the image.
7. Current page selling-point label or short phrase: at least one item that supports the page's core selling point.
8. Current page composition: full product, crop, ingredient, craft, scene, information card, collage, or similar.
9. Current page difference: state how this page differs from the previous page in background, subject scale, visual device, and title placement.
10. Negative constraints: do not fabricate sales, certifications, testing, reviews, medical efficacy, or brand authorization; do not use consultation-style CTA copy.


## Product Consistency Lock

Every prompt must reuse the same `Product Consistency Lock`, including at least:

- `silhouette lock`: preserve overall shape, width/height/depth proportions, rounded corners or edges, open/closed state, and product orientation.
- `pattern/logo placement lock`: preserve relative position and direction of patterns, logos, nameplates, labels, and visible copy; keep unreadable text as visual placement only and do not invent words.
- `shape/proportion lock`: preserve size relationships between the main body, accessories, base, handles, ports, and decorative parts.
- `relative position lock`: preserve top/bottom, left/right, front/back, surrounding, centered, edge-aligned, and other positional relationships between key parts.
- `color/material lock`: preserve primary color, secondary color, color-area ratio, and visible material appearance such as transparent, metallic, textile, or creamy surfaces.
- `do-not-change list`: explicitly forbid changing the product into another style from the same category, adding unprovided structures, deleting key patterns/nameplates, moving decorative elements, or changing the core silhouette.

Detail, material, craft, and scene pages may omit the full product. When a product crop appears, it must come from the same product and must not become a replacement product from the same category.
## Page Copy Rule

- Each screen should communicate only one core selling point.
- On-image copy should be short, clear, and in the buyer-facing language implied by the user's request; do not place long body text.
- Titles should prioritize buyer language over product-category names.
- Material, ingredient, craft, detail, and scene pages may omit the full product, but they must still include a title and selling-point label.
- Decorative text, garbled text, blank labels, or meaningless English do not count as valid selling-point copy.

## Design Strength Rule

- Establish and reuse a `Design Strength Lock` for the full prompt set. Do not rely only on generic words such as premium, clean, or modern.
- Every image must specify a clear visual device, such as speed lines, material magnifier, cutaway window, floating card, scene depth, exploded structure, ingredient installation, or hand operation.
- Every image must specify subject scale. In an eight-image set, no more than four images should use a large full-product hero.
- Every image must state how it differs from the previous page: change at least two of background system, product angle, title position, image/text ratio, or device type.
- Strong design should come from composition, light/shadow, scale, material evidence, and scene tension, not fabricated selling points.

## Layout Diversity Rule

An eight-image set must mix at least five structures:

- Hero poster.
- Scene immersion.
- Enlarged local detail.
- Ingredient or material flat lay.
- Craft/process flow.
- Parameter or specification information card.
- Comparison or objection-handling page.
- FAQ or closing summary.
- Usage process or hand-operation page.
- Category motif page, such as track, kitchen, bathroom, desk, living room, or outdoor setting.

Do not make every page follow the same centered-product, top-left-title, bottom-label structure.
