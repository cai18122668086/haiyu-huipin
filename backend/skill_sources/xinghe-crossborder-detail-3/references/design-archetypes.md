# Design Archetypes

Use this file when the user does not provide a visual style. Choose the closest category archetype, then adapt it to the product's visible materials, color, price impression, audience, and platform.

## Global Rules

- Use strong commercial art direction, not generic safe infographics.
- Keep the product accurate, but make the surrounding campaign design expressive.
- Show the selling point through composition, scene, macro detail, action, or contrast before relying on labels.
- Keep in-image text short. Prefer one bold headline plus 1-3 short labels.
- Do not repeat the same layout rhythm across the set.
- Do not invent certifications, test numbers, ratings, reviews, medical effects, safety guarantees, or platform badges.

## Sports / Outdoor / Travel

Visual language: kinetic motion, diagonal energy, speed trails, road/gym/outdoor context, impact crops, outsole or structure macro, bold athletic typography.

Use: running shoes, workout gear, backpacks, tents, bottles, luggage, outdoor accessories.

Strong modules:
- hero poster with product cutting through motion trails
- macro proof of outsole, grip pattern, fabric, strap, buckle, or structure
- lifestyle action scene with cropped body or hands
- scenario strip with road, gym, commute, trail, travel, or storage context
- final summary with bold product silhouette and minimal feature cards

Avoid: flat white catalog scene for every module, fake endurance data, waterproof claims unless provided, extreme sports scenes when the product is casual.

## Beauty / Makeup / Personal Care

Visual language: sensory macro, liquid/cream/powder texture, glow, reflection, premium negative space, editorial close-up, soft depth.

Use: skincare, makeup, hair care, fragrance, grooming tools.

Strong modules:
- glossy hero with product and texture smear/mist/drop
- macro applicator or texture proof
- routine scene on vanity, pouch, travel bag, bathroom shelf
- before/after-style visual only when no unsupported efficacy claim is implied
- final summary with product packshot and sensory labels

Avoid: medical claims, dermatologist-tested claims, ingredient claims not visible/provided, fake clinical diagrams.

## Fashion / Apparel

Visual language: editorial model crop, silhouette, fabric motion, styling scene, magazine typography, textile macro.

Use: clothing, shoes with fashion positioning, bags, accessories.

Strong modules:
- editorial hero crop with model or ghost mannequin
- fabric macro plus drape/silhouette composition
- movement or sitting comfort scene
- styling boards for work, travel, casual, party
- detail callouts for pockets, zipper, stitching, strap, fit

Avoid: inconsistent model identity, overusing full-face model shots, fake luxury branding, unsupported material names.

## Home / Kitchen / Storage

Visual language: warm lifestyle scene, real room context, scale proof, before/after organization, clean material close-up.

Use: home goods, kitchenware, storage, cleaning tools, furniture, decor.

Strong modules:
- hero in a believable room or counter scene
- scale-in-context with hands, shelf, cabinet, sink, table, or countertop
- problem-solution composition
- material/surface macro
- cleaning or use-action sequence

Avoid: impossible room scale, fake safety/load claims, too many floating icons.

## Electronics / Accessories

Visual language: precision grid, dark or clean tech studio, interface-style callouts, glow accents, exploded detail, desk/travel context.

Use: chargers, cables, cases, headphones, gadgets, desk accessories.

Strong modules:
- high-contrast tech hero
- port/button/interface macro
- compatibility scene using generic devices only
- setup sequence with numbered steps
- desk/travel/gaming/work context

Avoid: unsupported wattage, battery life, waterproof rating, benchmark scores, device compatibility lists unless provided.

## Food / Beverage / Supplements

Visual language: appetite-first hero, ingredient staging, serving moment, texture close-up, warm natural light.

Use: packaged food, drinks, snacks, coffee, tea, supplements.

Strong modules:
- appetizing hero serving scene
- texture or ingredient macro
- preparation sequence
- storage/portability context
- occasion scene: breakfast, office, gym bag, picnic, family table

Avoid: health treatment claims, nutrition numbers, organic/non-GMO/allergen-free claims unless provided.

## Pet / Baby / Kids

Visual language: soft emotional scene, comfort texture, parent/pet action, gentle colors, clear scale.

Use: pet supplies, baby products, toys, maternity, kids items.

Strong modules:
- emotional hero with pet/parent/child context
- size or fit proof
- material comfort macro
- cleaning or one-hand use scene
- home/travel scenario

Avoid: veterinary claims, guaranteed safety, non-toxic claims, age ranges, certifications unless provided.

## Auto / Tools / Hardware

Visual language: problem-solution scene, rugged material proof, high-contrast utility layout, installation sequence, component macro.

Use: car accessories, hardware, tools, repair products, industrial accessories.

Strong modules:
- problem solved hero
- compatibility caveat visual without inventing models
- component detail macro
- installation or hand-action sequence
- storage/included-parts summary if visible/provided

Avoid: vehicle model claims, safety certifications, torque/load ratings, waterproof/impact-proof ratings unless provided.
