# Visual audit

Judge pixels, not contracts. Record product prominence, headline rank, visible
physical evidence, depth, commercial closure, background competition, and the
thumbnail scan order before individual checks.

Also transcribe the visible headline and line count, then record the observed
composition archetype, product zone, copy zone, dominant view, evidence
topology, and one concrete visible difference from the previous page. These
observations must match the current page's machine contract; a page number or a
different sentence does not prove layout diversity.

Inspect the complete ordered quality set: product identity, factual copy,
typography style and legibility, page role, evidence relation, Campaign Style,
headline treatment, palette roles, graphic language, photography finish,
information surfaces, layout cloning, set diversity, product separation,
numeric hierarchy, design intensity, visual performance, commercial baseline,
approved-plan fidelity, Campaign Constitution, source-background isolation,
selling-point visibility and dominance, claim/evidence clarity, text/proof
balance, thumbnail success, unsupported claims, and hard errors.

Material blockers include identity or fact drift, model-invented claims,
unreadable or broken text, impossible geometry, source-background leakage,
failed page duty, missing core visual proof, weak selling-point dominance,
unclear claim-to-evidence relation, failed commercial-performance contract,
failed baseline, or failed thumbnail communication. Minor style preferences and
the mere presence of multiple selling points are advisory.

Judge source-background leakage by recognizable transfer, not by generic prop
category. A common unbranded cup, bean, towel, plant, utensil, or category-
appropriate surface is not a material failure merely because a source photo
also contained that kind of object. Block only when visible source-specific
branding, distinctive objects, arrangement, room geometry, capture artifacts,
or accessory grouping is recognizably reproduced, or when a prop could
materially imply that an unverified accessory is included with the product.

For claim auditing, authority comes from provenance rather than external proof.
Treat explicit user text and clearly readable copy in user-provided same-product
images as authorized facts, including efficacy, health or safety language,
numbers, endorsements, prices, offers, and rankings. Their lack of certificates,
reports, or links is not a defect and never triggers repair. Preserve any stated
number and condition exactly. Fail `no_unsupported_claim` only when the generated
page adds a factual proposition that is absent from the user fact pool or
materially strengthens one. A bounded paraphrase or low-risk advertising
rhetoric that adds no new factual proposition is at most advisory. Generated
copy never becomes authority for a later page.

The search cover uses eight axes: product dominance, headline performance,
physical evidence, spatial depth, commercial closure, thumbnail competitiveness,
commercial-frame structure, and type hierarchy. The mean must reach 80 and no
axis may be below 65.

Specifically reject the observed regression signature: a correct large product
with only one headline, decorative greenery/fabric/water, no unequal evidence
modules, weak numeric hierarchy, little depth, and no verified closure. A clean
or attractive page does not pass merely because identity and text are correct.

Reject a search headline that exceeds its compiled hard character or line
limit, uses a full multi-clause fact sentence, repeats its own meaning, or
shrinks typography to fit. Reject adjacent pages that retain the same product
side, copy side, dominant view, evidence topology, and spatial skeleton.

Ordinary repair edits the failed commercial draft and preserves passed layers.
Identity-destroying or impossible-geometry failures rebuild from original
same-product evidence because the failed draft is not a safe product canvas.
After a page's shared modification slot is consumed, retain residual issues for
final disclosure. An automatic repair failure continues the campaign; a failed
user-requested local revision stops for user intervention. Neither path permits
a second modification of that page in the current campaign.
