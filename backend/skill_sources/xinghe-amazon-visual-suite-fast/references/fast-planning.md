# Fast Planning Contract

The output is one complete analysis and one complete 21-row plan. The server supplies the canonical inventory and dimensions: square main (2000x2000 target), six vertical secondary (1200x1600 targets), seven wide desktop A+ (1464x600 targets) and seven 4:3 mobile A+ (1600x1200 targets). Every canvas is generated independently at its own ratio. Original provider dimensions are reported honestly and must satisfy the website's ratio and clarity validator; no crop, stretch, padding or upscaling may manufacture acceptance.

## Shared truth and strategy, written once

Choose one supplied white_background_product asset as primary_product_source_id. Describe that exact product, packaging version, silhouette, proportions, color/material appearance, visible structure, authentic logos/labels and physical scale in Product Master. Other sources may supply compatible detail, but never blend conflicting packaging or labels. Product photos, package/detail/brand evidence and explicitly provided product facts may establish truth within their scope. Competitor/style/reference sources only guide visual conventions. Never infer this SKU's specifications or copy a competitor's brand from them.

Classify information into confirmed, reasonable inference and needs confirmation. Keep uncertain, prohibited or unsupported claims off-image; no fabricated certifications, awards, ratings, rankings, endorsements, sales, test numbers, before/after efficacy curves, pricing or gifts. Do not invent material composition, parts, accessories or alternate SKUs. Use conservative visible evidence when data is missing.

Plan the buyer demand map and match each demand to a selling point. Establish one campaign palette, lighting, type mood, label style and information density. Freeze product consistency and layout-diversity strategies. Product identity and facts apply to every item without being repeated in each row.

## Individual image decisions

For each canonical id, author its buyer question, one dominant selling point, information confidence, visual proof, exact shopper-facing headline/subcopy, detailed layout direction, exact source role labels needed and only item-specific risk notes. Describe subject/copy positions, scale, hierarchy and how visual evidence conveys the point before the copy is read. Do not emit generic template placeholders or repeat a shared guard paragraph in every row.

Main: use the real primary product source, clean surrounding background to white, preserve authentic branding and readable existing label text. Add no new headline, subcopy, icon, badge, rating, lifestyle props, invented accessories or fake packaging. Output empty headline and subcopy. Imperfect source background alone is not a reason to invent a replacement product.

Secondary sequence: core benefit; pain-point/use context without invented result data; function/structure with visible proof; size/fit/compatibility with only known measurements; use steps appropriate to the observed product; trust/package recap with only supplied contents.

Desktop A+ sequence: brand/product opening, buyer demand, core feature, real-life scenario, detail trust, conservative choice/comparison, closing recap. Avoid fake competitor claims. Each wide composition uses its own evidence hierarchy, with important text inside the center safe area and clear wide-screen pacing.

Mobile A+ keeps the corresponding seven selling purposes but reauthors its own composition: larger focal subject, shorter headline, sparse labels, readable macro or structure evidence, obvious scale/context, simplified comparison and uncluttered recap. Never say 'crop desktop', 'resize desktop' or use identical desktop/mobile layout descriptions.

Use varied hero, scene, macro, structure, use-process, package, scale and recap compositions; at most three large-product-plus-top-title layouts across the selling set. Avoid busy grids, tiny copy, generic AI cards, random glow, fake badges and repeated layouts. Keep critical product/copy inside the central 86%; wide A+ text clear of the outer 6% sides and 8% top/bottom. Preserve believable contact, shadows, perspective, occlusion, grip and gravity.

## Delivery semantics

All 21 ids are required. A contact sheet never counts as one of the 21. The runtime verifies image decodability, original byte identity, clarity, ratio, provenance, canonical item ownership, immutable plan and Skill versions, and inventory completeness. It performs no routine semantic image review, so the truthful review label is NOT_REVIEWED. Missing or invalid images are partial/failed and remain eligible for explicit failed-item recovery; no AI audit or automatic aesthetic redraw is added.
