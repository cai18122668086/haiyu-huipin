# Cross-category Design Intelligence v0.1.11

## Authority and scope

This reference is an engineered visual prior derived from a full local review
of 478 images and 1,790 semantic panels across beauty, home/daily cleaning,
digital, small appliances, automotive, fashion/accessories, medical/health,
mother/baby and pet supplies. It is not factual authority for any product.

Preserve the v0.1.7 order:

`USER_FACT → EVIDENCE_DERIVED → EXPERIMENTAL_PATTERN → ENGINEERED_DEFAULT → MODEL_FREEDOM`.

Never transfer a source brand, logo, SKU, product color, shape, component,
claim, number, price, certification, person, animal, scene or page sequence.
The archive can nominate reasoning and visual grammar only. The current
same-product evidence remains the sole identity and supported-view authority.

## Universal compiler order

For each atomic claim, resolve:

1. the verified fact and buyer objection;
2. what must be visible without copy;
3. the current product form and exact evidence-bearing target;
4. the highest-scoring supported view;
5. the real product/subject/action/state relation;
6. one primary proof, one secondary clarifier and optional tertiary trust cue;
7. the evidence topology and page-specific spatial skeleton;
8. copy duty, result boundary and claim-job typography;
9. one thumbnail success condition;
10. one explicit difference from the adjacent page.

Layout is the result of this reasoning. Do not select a fixed template first.
Do not pass a method menu or abstract adjective to the image model.

## Claim observability

- `DIRECTLY_VISIBLE`: current color, silhouette, material, texture, structure,
  component, interface, fit, length, opening, pocket, granule, clump or state.
- `ACTION_DEMONSTRABLE`: real dispense, operate, install, switch, adjust,
  fold, wear, load, feed, clean, rinse, maintain or care action.
- `SENSORY_PROXY_ONLY`: scent, taste, softness, comfort, coolness or
  after-feel; allow one subordinate material-compatible cue, never fake proof.
- `TEXT_ONLY_VERIFIED`: formula, ingredient, technology, duration,
  compatibility, age boundary, size, nutrition, test or certification.
- `RESULT_NOT_VISUALLY_PROVABLE_WITH_CURRENT_EVIDENCE`: treatment,
  improvement, whitening, repair, guaranteed persistence, antibacterial,
  deodorizing, low-dust or other results without current result evidence.

Split compound copy into atomic claim units. Do not let one attractive image
silently prove several unrelated claims.

## Universal evidence grammar

Prefer:

`current product → claim-defining action/component/material/scene → visible
state → bounded result or complete text duty`.

Use people, animals and environments only when they prove action, scale, fit,
contact, target or verified use context. Remove decorative subjects.

Every pointer, line, macro, highlight and label must land on the exact visible
current-product component or relation it names. If the target is unsupported,
hidden or ambiguous, remove the device and retain complete fact-bound copy.

Line art, wireframes, empty icons, fake laboratory imagery, decorative light
arcs and equal-weight card walls cannot replace primary or secondary proof.

## Product-form category priors

These priors nominate evidence candidates; they never create facts or fix a
palette, angle, page order or layout.

### Beauty and personal care

Resolve spray, pump/dispenser, dropper, tube, jar, applicator, sheet/box and
fluid forms separately. Route texture, application, sensory, ingredient,
technology and bounded-result claims independently. Start scent at a verified
use/contact point; only a verified atomizing product may originate a real
spray from its nozzle. Keep ingredient cues subordinate and forbid visible
microcapsules, molecules, penetration paths and unsupported before/after.

### Home and daily cleaning

Use real target contact, visible foam/film/residue, wiping or rinse and only a
bounded result. Separate cleaning strength, fragrance/care, material safety,
method and offer. Do not use arrows or icons as dirt-removal evidence.

### Digital electronics

Bind performance to the current interface, control, port, component, screen,
wear relation or operating state. Place verified numbers next to the object
they describe. Metal, textile, clear shell, plastic and glass require distinct
light/material treatment; "technology" never implies a default blue palette.

### Small appliances

Resolve cleaning, kitchen, environmental, personal-care and portable task
forms. Prefer real working interfaces: brush/target contact, airflow outlet,
water path, blade/head, container, filter, battery, control or maintenance
action. Effects may clarify but cannot replace the working relation.

### Automotive

Separate vehicle fragrance from mechanical mount/charging forms.

- fragrance: establish the current bottle/housing, wick or wood-stop contact,
  hanging/placement relation and a subordinate verified scent-source cue;
- mount/charger: establish installation position and scale, then bind
  stability, angle, reach, compatibility or charging to the exact base,
  joint, contact and device relation.

Magnetic fields, energy rings and device arrays are explanatory only.

### Fashion and accessories

Resolve footwear, apparel, soft accessories and carry systems separately.
Start with a complete silhouette, worn state or complete product, then add one
fit/action/loading/material/construction proof. Treat coolness, drying,
sun-protection, warmth and waterproofing as visible, demonstrable, tested or
sensory according to current evidence. Water, snow, rock and landscape
metaphors never prove function.

### Medical and health

Compile current form/package/device, verified use, audience, evidence grade
and risk/result boundary separately. Source, testing and certification must be
real and readable. Clinical blue-white, body diagrams, doctors, seals and
large result numbers do not create authority. Never strengthen diagnostic,
treatment or guaranteed-result language.

### Mother and baby

Separate experience, function and trust. Establish verified age, contact area
and use boundary before atmosphere. A caregiver or child must prove age,
scale, contact, care action, movement or fit. Durable products require exact
wheels, joints, guards, supports, locks, interfaces and task relations.
Pastels, clouds, bubbles and cartoons prove neither safety nor efficacy.

### Pet supplies

Resolve food, litter, care/cleansing, supplement and device forms separately.

- food: source/material → piece/granule texture → feeding relation → verified
  nutrition/process boundary;
- litter: granule → dust/odor claim boundary → clump → cleaning task;
- cleansing: contact/foam → cleaning → rinse → bounded comfort/safety copy;
- device: interface/component → animal scale/use → maintenance.

The animal must participate in feeding, toileting, care, interaction or scale;
it cannot be a decorative mascot.

## Campaign Style compilation

Freeze one style for all five pages, but derive it from the current product:

- explicit current product colors and materials;
- shape, surface and visible contents;
- verified function, claim and use scene;
- audience, offer status and user preference;
- category only as a weak scene/material prior.

When no explicit current-product color is available, use a neutral unbound
palette rather than a category stereotype. Never default digital to blue,
medical to clinical blue-white, mother/baby to pastel, automotive fragrance to
warm gold, automotive accessories to blue-black, fashion to outdoor hard type
or pet products to cartoon color.

Freeze typography roles, palette roles, graphic geometry, information-surface
geometry, photography finish and density band. Vary page view, crop, product
scale, state, scene geometry, subject anchor, reading path and proof topology.

Typography follows the claim job and current product. Performance, mechanism,
editorial/sensory, friendly care, verified data and offer numbers may nominate
different treatments, but packaging cues and legibility may reject every
nomination. Preserve complete Chinese phrases and semantic line breaks.

## Five-page rhythm

One Campaign does not imply one skeleton. A valid set normally covers five
different purchase tasks selected from identity, operation, scene, material,
structure, fit, maintenance, trust, specification or offer. The current
purchase logic may reorder the middle pages.

Use a unique shot key per page: view/crop, scale, product state, scene
geometry, subject anchor and layout family. Do not repeat the same centered
complete-product frame with different copy.

## Stop conditions

Block or simplify when:

- the product is no longer the first fixation;
- an identity-defining edge, color, part or label changes;
- a claim is understandable only from copy or decoration;
- a subject does not prove a real relation;
- a pointer lands on the wrong component;
- a visual device implies a technology, test, result or authority not present
  in USER_FACT and current evidence;
- adjacent pages reuse the same complete-product frame or evidence topology.
