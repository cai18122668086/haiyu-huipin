# Evidence Representation Router

Choose how verified evidence is shown after product facts, page role, and layout are known.

## Routing principles

1. Product identity comes from current original references.
2. Visible claims come from `USER_FACT` or safe compression only.
3. Representation mode never authorizes a new fact.
4. Use icons for navigation or abstraction, not as a universal replacement for required visual evidence.
5. Omit a visual claim when its evidence cannot be represented safely.

## Food defaults

- identity: full-product commercial photography;
- feature: product plus real food macro;
- scene/instructions: real action-sequence photography;
- component proof: real fact-bound food close-ups in varied photo windows;
- specification: product plus numeric specification poster;
- offer: product plus verified numeric offer hierarchy.

Do not invent sachet appearance, amount, ratio, ingredient grade, serving result, or efficacy. A food close-up visualizes a verified component; it does not prove an unobserved package form.

## Other category defaults

- furniture: visible structure/material macro, real use scene, source-bound component close-ups;
- electronics: visible interface/function macro, real use scene, source-bound detail grid;
- small appliances: visible operation macro and real operation sequence;
- beauty: verified texture macro, real application sequence, package/applicator details;
- general: product hero, source-bound macro, real use scene, independent evidence grid.

All category routes are `ENGINEERED_DEFAULT` and yield to `USER_FACT` and `EVIDENCE_DERIVED`.
