# Optional Transaction Module for Search Main Image v0.1.12

This module converts verified transaction facts into an optional compact module inside the search main image. It does not define the search page, replace any of the five carousel pages, or create a promotional close page. It is a fact-bound expression prior, not a fact source or reusable color theme.

## Activation gate

- Read only `product.verified_offers` as USER_FACT.
- Activate only when at least one offer is concrete enough to render without guessing and materially helps the search cover qualify a click.
- Keep vague phrases such as “限时优惠” out of the visual framework until the discount, quantity, price, eligibility, gift, audience or time condition is supplied.
- Do not treat sample-board brands, prices, rankings, dates, gifts, colors or campaign names as evidence.
- Ranking or sales claims additionally require a named source and time scope.

## Open routing sequence

Reason in this order:

1. Which verified transaction fact, if any, improves search-page click qualification?
2. Which eligibility, quantity, threshold, time, audience or gift conditions must remain attached?
3. Which current-product view preserves identity while leaving a readable transaction band?
4. Which single skeleton best expresses that fact structure?
5. Which small device—price anchor, coupon card, gift card, time chip, quantity ladder or service row—is actually justified?

The choice remains open-ended. The named skeletons are compositional families, not fixed templates.

## Transferable skeleton families

- Product hero + quantity ladder + bottom transaction band.
- Product hero + coupon condition + bottom transaction band.
- Product hero + price anchor + condition band.
- Product hero + bound gift module + transaction band.
- Product hero + rebate/payment equation + condition band.
- Product hero + one time/audience chip + transaction band.
- Product hero + verified service/trust row.
- Product hero + one verified livestream action badge; the badge never becomes the page style.

## Set-level style rule

The search main image inherits the frozen Campaign Style derived from the current product: palette roles, typography families, photography finish, surface material and shared geometry. Its optional transaction module may increase contrast around one anchor. It may not default to festival red-gold, livestream blue, beauty pink or any sample-board palette.

## Information hierarchy

1. Complete current product and one strongest verified transaction hook.
2. Complete eligibility, threshold, quantity, gift or time condition.
3. Only already-verified trust or service facts.

The product remains visible at commercial scale. Graphic bands clarify the offer; they never replace the product. Avoid equal-weight badge walls.

## Live-room component board

Treat isolated livestream badges as a vocabulary of small devices only. Use one only when the exact corresponding fact exists, such as membership, mystery box, seconds-kill, limited quantity, follow reward or red-packet event. Never inherit the board’s blue palette, mascots or livestream context automatically.

## Copy and compliance

- Preserve offer wording and conditions without mechanical truncation.
- Never invent price, gift, ranking, deadline, stock limit, channel, audience, certification or service.
- Do not strengthen the deal beyond USER_FACT.
- Keep Product Identity, USER_FACT, compliance, factual-copy and post-generation visual gates unchanged from v0.1.7.
- Before rendering a price equation, verify that every component is stackable and that the arithmetic reaches the stated final price.
- Before rendering a gift module, require gift name, quantity/specification and eligibility; keep any time or stock boundary attached.
- Before rendering a preorder module, keep deposit, final payment, expansion rule, stage time and final payable amount as one chain; do not show a partial chain as a complete deal.
- Before rendering ranking, sales, cashback, mystery-box or free-order claims, require the source, time scope and applicable rule; these are secondary proof and cannot independently activate a promotional page.
