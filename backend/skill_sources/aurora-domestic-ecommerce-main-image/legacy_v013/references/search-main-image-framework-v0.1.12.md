# Search Main Image Framework v0.1.12

## Role in the six-image set

Output one search/recommendation listing cover followed by the existing five
carousel pages. The search cover earns a qualified click; the five carousel
pages retain their deeper identity, feature, proof, scene, specification, trust
or value jobs. Do not sacrifice a carousel page to create the search cover.

## Universal job

At mobile thumbnail scale, make the current product immediately recognizable
and communicate one strongest verified reason to click. Use this hierarchy:

1. one complete current-product hero with unmistakable identity;
2. one strongest verified current-product value hook;
3. at most one compact verified audience, transaction or trust module when it
   materially improves click qualification.

The page may succeed without promotion. Search main image and promotional
poster are not synonyms.

## Open reasoning sequence

`current product identity → likely list-page buyer intent → strongest verified click hook → proof visible at thumbnail scale → optional verified qualifier → unique search-cover composition`

Select, combine or reject expression candidates. Do not use a fixed industry
template, fixed page skeleton, default palette, default typography or inherited
competitor composition.

## Optional information modules

- Core value: select one verified advantage from current-product purchase logic.
- Audience or scene: use only when supplied by USER_FACT and useful for filtering.
- Transaction: activate only from concrete `product.verified_offers`; retain all
  eligibility, threshold, quantity, time and audience conditions.
- Trust: use only verified ranking, sales, qualification, service, authenticity
  or warranty facts with every required source and scope boundary.

No module may invent a fact or obscure Product Identity. Avoid equal-weight
badge walls and detail-page density.

## Difference from carousel page 1

Both pages may show a complete hero, but they must not share an exact or
near-duplicate shot. Require a different view or crop, product scale, state or
subject relation, scene geometry, reading path and evidence topology. The search
cover optimizes fast list-page recognition; carousel page 1 remains the richer
identity entry after the click.

## Campaign Style

Freeze one current-product Campaign Style before the search cover. Share its
photography finish, palette roles, typography families, information-surface
geometry and graphic language across all six pages. Do not share layout.

## Thumbnail and compliance gate

- Recognize category and current-product identity before reading small copy.
- Read one value hook during a fast mobile list-page scan.
- Keep the product stronger than labels, cards, badges and decoration.
- Keep copy short, complete and naturally broken; never mechanically truncate Chinese.
- Do not invent price, offer, ranking, qualification, audience, service or trust.
- Apply platform-specific size, prohibited-word and advertising-compliance rules
  when the target platform is known; do not claim universal platform compliance.
