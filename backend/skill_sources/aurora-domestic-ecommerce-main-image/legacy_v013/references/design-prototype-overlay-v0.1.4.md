# Design Prototype Overlay v0.1.4

## Position in the compiler

`Raw product facts → Planner → Product Identity + Compliance + Copy Integrity → Campaign Design → Design Prototype Overlay → generation_prompt`

The overlay is an independent non-factual visual layer. Product identity, user facts, verified offers, compliance, and complete copy phrases always have priority.

## Source governance

Priority:

`USER_FACT → EVIDENCE_DERIVED → EXPERIMENTAL_PATTERN → ENGINEERED_DEFAULT → MODEL_FREEDOM`

The following prototype research artifacts were used for design grammar and engineering structure only:

- v1 typography, campaign rhythm, background/scene, and surface grammar: `EXPERIMENTAL_PATTERN` or `NON_BINDING_REFERENCE`;
- v2 frozen bundle and integration plan: governance and field-structure reference;
- v2.1 promotion decisions and frozen bundle: authoritative evidence boundary.

The v2.1 bundle explicitly blocks real carousel rhythm and furniture promotion. Therefore `IDENTITY_EDITORIAL`, `FEATURE_MACRO`, `SCENE_AIRY`, `DETAIL_TECH_GRID`, and `OFFER_NUMERIC_HERO` are all `ENGINEERED_DEFAULT` in v0.1.4. No food, beauty, appliance, or other category color, copy, product fact, or weak generated visual is carried into the overlay.

## Overlay-owned fields

- layout archetype and reading path;
- product occupancy and anchor;
- composition axis;
- title, supporting-copy, and evidence/offer regions;
- semantic line-break plan derived from complete fact-bound copy;
- typography ratios and keyword emphasis method;
- graphic-device family and limit;
- background material and lighting structure;
- explicit page-to-page difference.

## Hard boundaries

- The overlay cannot change product colors, silhouette, components, geometry, material identity, logo, accessories, or view evidence.
- It cannot create or rewrite claims, offers, parameters, certifications, efficacy, compatibility, or rights.
- It cannot split Chinese copy by fixed character count.
- It cannot use earlier generated product images as a visual target.
- When layout conflicts with product identity, reduce decoration complexity.
- `generation_performed=false` until a separate user confirmation.
