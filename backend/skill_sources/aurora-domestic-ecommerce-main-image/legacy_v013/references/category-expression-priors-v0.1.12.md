# User-validated category expression priors v0.1.12

## Authority and boundary

These priors record why several user-accepted outcomes communicated clearly.
They are optional reasoning aids, not hard templates and not product evidence.
They cannot supply a brand, SKU, claim, number, shade, offer, package count,
garment structure, product proportion, page sequence, composition, palette,
font, camera or copy.

Resolve every page in this order:

`current product form → verified claim or objection → proof required → same-product evidence → supported view and component → select, combine, revise or reject a prior → compile a unique page`

Any candidate may be omitted. A new expression is preferred when it proves the
current product more directly.

## Pet litter

Useful optional relations include:

- a complete current package paired with real animal-to-litter scale or use;
- current granules shown through a truthful pour, scoop or observable dust state;
- a real clump bound to scoop, lift, release or basin contact;
- material or odor reasoning bound to a real use scene and a clear claim boundary;
- a verified bundle shown with exact current package count, or as text-only when
  a count-faithful product arrangement cannot be maintained.

Keep current package geometry, print zones, variant markings and granule form.
Derive type force and accents from that package. A decorative animal is not
proof; category convention cannot invent dust, odor, clumping or bundle facts.

## Apparel

Useful optional relations include:

- one complete worn view that establishes current silhouette and fit;
- a real coverage or protection action bound to the exact garment parts;
- material macro paired with the same garment and a bounded sensory claim;
- rear, pocket, pleat, seam or ventilation details bound to their true locations;
- several verified scenes only when action, view and proof duty genuinely differ.

Keep current length, fit, closure, hood, brim, face cover, cuff, pocket, pleat
and seam geometry. Typography should belong to the garment, audience and scene;
default heavy pure-black headlines are rejected when they fight a light or soft
material character. Metaphor alone cannot prove a function.

## Lip color and applicator products

Useful optional relations include:

- one complete current container as the identity anchor;
- current applicator plus product smear for texture and finish;
- one-to-one verified shade, swatch and label binding;
- real lip or cheek application without turning use into an efficacy result;
- verified audience or scene guidance paired with bound shade evidence.

Preserve current length-to-diameter ratio, cap/body partition, diameter, band
and label placement. When source views support them, preserve the applicator,
neck and opening relation as well. Derive editorial tone and text color from the
current package and shades; never reuse example shade codes, names or colors.

## Explicit non-template contract

The priors set no required page count, page order, layout archetype, palette,
typeface, copy formula, product pose or camera. Five pages may share one frozen
Campaign Style while using entirely different evidence topology, view, action
and composition. Current-product evidence always outranks the prior.
