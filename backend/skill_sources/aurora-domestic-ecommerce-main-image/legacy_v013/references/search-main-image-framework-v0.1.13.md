# Search Main Image Framework v0.1.13

## Role

Output one search/recommendation listing cover before the preserved five-page
carousel. Its job is to earn a qualified click in a fast list scan. It is not a
detail page, a lifestyle poster or automatically a festival promotion poster.

## Required commercial frame

Use these as working bands, not fixed coordinates:

- safe frame or coherent container edge: 2%–4%;
- current-brand/store header: 6%–10%;
- product and message zone: 58%–72%;
- bottom closure: 16%–24% with a verified transaction, otherwise 10%–16%;
- complete product external bounds: about 42%–58% of the canvas and 50%–70%
  of canvas height when product form permits.

Inside the central zone, let the product carry about 50%–65%, the click hook
20%–30%, and auxiliary modules 8%–18%. Build two or three clear scan stops:
product/hook, one evidence anchor, then the closure band. These ratios may move
when current-product form, legibility or identity evidence requires it.

## Open layout families

Select, combine or synthesize rather than trace a sample:

- complete hero with a side hook and bottom closure;
- centered hero with a price, specification or benefit anchor;
- complete hero with one fact-bound gift or offer card;
- split hero and short proof field;
- complete hero with a service or trust closure.

Curves, corners, cutouts, overlaps and containers derive from current-product
form and surfaces. Rounded products may support curves and soft cards; hard
devices may use straighter cuts; transparent products may support restrained
translucent information surfaces. Category is only a prior.

## Information hierarchy

1. Current brand/store identity.
2. Complete recognizable current product.
3. One strongest verified click reason.
4. Zero to three short verified proof, specification, transaction or trust
   modules chosen by relevance.
5. One bottom closure that completes the commercial read.

Use distinct typography roles for headline, numeric anchor, evidence and
closure. Do not use one undifferentiated plain-black style. Keep no more than
three dominant visual anchors.

## Offer routing

When a concrete `product.verified_offers` fact exists, the bottom band may use
one dominant transaction anchor and at most two secondary conditions. Keep
threshold, quantity, eligibility, time and audience boundaries attached. Do
not show conflicting price mechanisms or arithmetic that does not reconcile.

Without a verified offer, keep the commercial frame. Increase product share,
shrink the closure band and use only verified specification, service,
authenticity, warranty, audience or core-performance facts. Never invent a
countdown, ranking, price, gift, coupon or sales figure to imitate a reference.

## Current-product style derivation

A useful starting role split is 65%–75% product-neutral ground, 15%–25%
current-product main color and 5%–10% transaction or attention color. It is not
a mandatory palette. Derive actual colors from the current product, verified
function and offer state. Red-gold, livestream blue, beauty pink, wellness
green and food orange are never defaults.

## Failure gates

Reject a search cover when it:

- reads as an airy lifestyle poster or a carousel detail page;
- makes the product a small prop or lets modules cover product identity;
- lacks both a brand identity region and a commercial closure region;
- uses one flat text treatment with no headline/numeric/evidence hierarchy;
- copies sample facts, brand, palette, exact layout or product pose;
- uses long detail-page explanations or an equal-weight badge wall;
- invents or overstates promotion, ranking, service, certification or trust;
- mechanically truncates Chinese.

## Set relation

Share one frozen Campaign Style across all six pages: photography finish,
palette roles, typography families, information-surface geometry and graphic
language. Do not share the same complete shot or layout. The search cover is
the list-page commercial entry; carousel page 1 remains the post-click identity
entry and must use a different view, crop, product scale, reading path and
evidence topology.
