# Search-cover commercial grammar v0.1.16

Use this module only for page 0. It operationalizes the earlier search-cover
corpus analysis and the user-approved Joyoung HU108 correction. Transfer the
commercial relationships; never copy a prior product's brand, claims, palette,
pose, props or exact coordinates.

## Why this exists

The failed Joyoung draft had correct product style but read as a pale carousel
or lifestyle image. The approved correction restored the missing list-page
grammar: identity header, strong hook, dominant complete product, short proof
modules, physical use evidence, transaction/specification closure and trust
strip. A later Agent must not reduce this to “thumbnail readable” or an abstract
score. The compiler must place the explicit blueprint in the image-generation
request and the audit must compare pixels to it.

## Required semantic zones

- Identity/header: 6%–10%, current brand/store/authenticity only.
- Product/message field: 58%–72%; complete product bounds usually 42%–58% of
  canvas and 55%–75% of height when form permits.
- Bottom commercial closure: 16%–24% with a complete verified offer; otherwise
  10%–16% using verified specification, audience, service or trust.
- Safe framing/container edge: 2%–4% when the product-derived style supports it.

The zones are semantic, not fixed rectangles. They may overlap, curve, cut or
interlock according to current-product form. All must remain visibly legible.

## Required scan and type hierarchy

Use two or three ordered scan stops: dominant product/strong hook, a grouped
physical-proof field, then commercial closure. The grouped field may contain
multiple related selling points or useful verified information modules when
they are visibly unequal and support the click decision. Use at least four visibly different type
roles across brand identity, hook or numeric anchor, evidence, commercial
conclusion and trust closure. Same-size plain text blocks fail even when the
words are correct.

Do not delete a useful identity, quantity, specification, offer, service, trust
or unit-information bar merely to reduce the number of selling points. Multiple
claims are allowed; unclear hierarchy is the defect, not information count.

## Physical proof

At least one current-product component, material, content, action, interface,
working state, use result or correctly related prop must carry the click reason.
Icons, snowflakes, air arcs, glows, callouts and written labels are secondary;
they never count as the proof by themselves. Cover all copy during audit. If a
directly visible or action-demonstrable hook disappears, rebuild the page.

## Offer routing

With a complete verified offer, allow one dominant transaction anchor and at
most two attached conditions. Without one, preserve the commercial frame but
close with verified specification, audience, service, authenticity or core
performance. Never invent price theater, ranking, countdown, gifts or sales.

## Failure signatures

- airy lifestyle poster, ordinary studio shot or carousel identity page;
- small product used as a scene prop;
- missing identity/header or bottom closure;
- headline rendered as a weak caption;
- three equal cards with no dominant message;
- correct selling-point words with no matching physical evidence;
- generic blue technology, red-gold festival or copied sample palette;
- a visually handsome page that does not implement the approved blueprint.

## Blind six-page role test

After all six pages pass individually, inspect them together with filenames and
order hidden. Exactly one image must be confidently identifiable as the search
cover. If the reviewer can only guess page 0, or a feature/parameter page is
equally plausible, the search cover is not sufficiently distinct. Strengthen
page 0's identity/header, dominant product-hook field, commercial closure and
type hierarchy; do not merely add a “主图” label.
