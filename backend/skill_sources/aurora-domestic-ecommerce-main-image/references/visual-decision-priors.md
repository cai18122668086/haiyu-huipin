# Visual decision priors

Use priors as candidate evidence relations, never as templates. Resolve in this
order:

`product form → verified claim/buyer objection → proof required → same-product
evidence → supported view/component/action → concrete composition`

For each page record one buyer objection, lead benefit, eligible source facts,
identity evidence, thumbnail success and one difference from the previous page.
Composition and camera notes are hypotheses, not tracing instructions.

Before layout, classify selected claims as directly visible,
action-demonstrable, contextually expressible, verified-text-only, or not
renderable. A text-only claim may appear as accurate copy. Define the benefit a
viewer should feel before selecting the shot; allow direct proof, result,
context, sensory expression and credible visual metaphor.

Derive the campaign from current-product cues: form rhythm, material boundary,
working interface, component relation, contact action, and credible use space.
Name the background rationale and the shortcut it avoids. “Blue gradient,”
“technology blue,” equal floating cards, detached arrows, and decorative energy
arcs are not rationales. They are defaults and compilation must reject them.

## View selection

Score evidence-supported candidates by identity clarity 30%, target visibility
20%, complete-product coverage 15%, component separation 10%, credible action or
scene relation 10%, and compositional harmony 15%.

## Product-form candidates

- Food: complete package, real product/ingredient macro, truthful consumption or
  preparation action, exact bundle/specification facts.
- Beauty: package/applicator identity, real texture, bounded application action,
  verified shade-to-swatch binding.
- Electronics/appliances: real controls, ports, working interfaces, component,
  maintenance, or target-contact relations before performance effects.
- Apparel/accessories: complete silhouette or worn state, then fit, construction,
  material, loading, or action proof.
- Seating/storage/durable goods: complete structure, exact components, capacity,
  access, adjustment, transformation, or verified use relation.
- Pet products: the animal must prove feeding, toileting, care, interaction, or
  scale rather than decorate the page.

## User-validated prior format

Persist transferable learning as: product-form signature, claim/evidence class,
buyer objection, identity invariants, candidate evidence relations, typography
or material tendency, forbidden shortcuts, observed success condition, failure
codes, and user-approval scope. Never persist example brand, SKU, copy, number,
palette, page order, complete layout, pose, or camera as authority.

The validated commercial baseline from prior successful domestic e-commerce
runs is a quality prior, not a user checkpoint: square listing surface, current
product as the dominant subject, two or three scan stops, visible commercial
closure, strong but controlled Chinese hierarchy, natural product angle, and
page-specific evidence. Do not copy the example brand, palette, offer, camera
or layout.

## Search cover

Use a complete current-product hero, one strongest verified click hook, zero to
three short relevant modules, and one commercial closure band. Keep two or three
scan stops at thumbnail scale. Use a different shot, crop, reading path, and
evidence topology from carousel page 1. Activate transaction modules only from
complete verified offer facts.

For every search cover hard-lock a 1:1 canvas. Reject portrait poster output.
If no offer is verified, close with verified specification, service, audience
or trust facts rather than removing the commercial frame.

For a complete-product hero, prefer `PRODUCT_PRESERVING_EDIT`: select one
same-product image as the edit target, lock its structure/proportions/components/
color, and permit only declared background, crop, layout, and copy changes.
Supporting views may check hidden details but may not be blended into a new SKU.
