# Visual strategy

Resolve each page as:

`product form → buyer objection → selected facts → observability → required
proof → supported same-product evidence → page-specific shot and composition`

Freeze Campaign Style across photography finish, light direction, color
temperature, palette roles and usage ratio, typography character and numeric
hierarchy, graphic language, information surfaces, density band, and material
finish. Vary
view, crop, product scale, product state, scene geometry, subject anchor,
reading path, evidence topology, and layout family by page.

Campaign Style controls finish, not spatial cloning. Obey each page's compiled
layout contract for composition archetype, product zone, copy zone, dominant
view, and evidence topology. Style calibration cannot overwrite these fields.

Separate source claim from display copy. Keep the complete fact in the plan,
then compress it into a short headline without strengthening its meaning. Move
the remaining clauses into a subheadline or supporting modules.

Classify claims as `DIRECTLY_VISIBLE`, `ACTION_DEMONSTRABLE`,
`TEXT_ONLY_VERIFIED`, or `NOT_RENDERABLE`. Unknown claims are not automatically
action-demonstrable; choose the conservative class supported by the fact.

Use category priors as candidates, never templates. Match the product rather
than a substring inside a claim; `洗衣液` is household cleaning, not apparel, and
a tea fragrance does not make it food. Product-specific evidence and user
preferences outrank category words. Reject generic blue technology,
festival red-gold, equal-card walls, floating parameters, decorative people,
and icons or light effects that replace real proof.

The image prompt must carry current-page identity locks, selected facts, buyer
takeaway, visual world, required proof, type hierarchy, reading path, product/
headline/evidence/depth/closure/tension targets, source-background isolation,
and creative degrees of freedom. For page 0, it also carries semantic search
zones, unequal module candidates, four-or-more type roles, and an anti-sparse
failure rule. Keep raw machine structures in the generation request, but do not
compress their visible consequences into vague phrases.
