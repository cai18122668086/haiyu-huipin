# Commercial performance baseline

This is the functional-quality floor inherited from successful pre-v2 output.
Transfer the relationships, not an example brand, palette, product pose, copy,
camera, or exact coordinates.

## Search listing cover

The search cover is a high-density commercial composition, not an editorial
carousel hero. Preserve these semantic zones while allowing them to overlap,
curve, cut, or interlock:

- identity/header: 6%–10%;
- product and dominant-message field: 58%–72%;
- bottom commercial closure: 16%–24% with a complete verified offer, otherwise
  10%–16% using verified specification, audience, service, trust, or condition;
- optional safe frame: 2%–4% when product-derived style supports it.

The complete product normally occupies 55%–75% of canvas height and is first or
joint-first with the hook. Use two or three ordered scan stops: product/strong
hook, real proof or unequal information modules, then closure. The page may use
zero to five unequal verified modules. Keep useful brand, numeric, efficacy,
specification, quantity, service, trust, and condition information when its
hierarchy is clear.

Use at least four visibly different type roles: identity, strong hook or number,
evidence, commercial/specification conclusion, and trust/condition note. A
headline must participate in composition rather than float as a caption.

The search headline is a compressed click hook, not the full source fact. Aim
for 4–10 Chinese characters or equivalent, never exceed 12 effective
characters or two lines, and move remaining verified meaning into one short
subheadline or unequal evidence modules. Do not shrink type to force a complete
sentence into the headline. Preserve exact numbers and conditions wherever
they are displayed.

At least one real current-product material, component, content, action, state,
result, comparison, or credible use relation must carry the click reason. Icons,
arrows, glows, botanical decoration, and written labels are secondary.

Build credible depth through foreground entry, midground product, background
context, contact shadow, double scale, or real use relation. Create visual
tension through overlap, interlock, diagonal, triangle, scale contrast, motion,
result relation, or controlled asymmetry. Do not regress to a product plus one
headline, an airy natural poster, an ordinary studio shot, or a sparse lifestyle
image.

## Carousel pages

- Identity hero: lower density than search, but retain a confident headline,
  dominant product, and a truthful scene, material, hand, content, or structural
  relation.
- Core proof: use real macro/full-product double scale, action, state, material,
  content, or result. The benefit direction survives copy removal.
- Scene/operation: show correct contact among product, person/hand, target object,
  and space; decorative people do not count.
- Detail/trust: use a real macro, structure, label, interface, material, service,
  or trust object with a clear claim relation.
- Closure: maintain visual strength while resolving verified specification,
  compatibility, audience, service, trust, or complete offer.

## Creative permission

The executor may use commercial dramatization, sensory atmosphere, scale
contrast, motion, before/after relation, result scenes, double scale, transparent
material windows, controlled metaphor, and product-derived graphic surfaces.
These devices amplify verified meaning; they cannot invent efficacy, numbers,
certification, structure, components, experiments, or offers.

## Visible failure signatures

- correct product but only one or two lines of copy and a decorative background;
- missing numeric hierarchy, evidence modules, conditions, or closure despite
  available verified information;
- botanical, fabric, water, person, icon, or macro detail unrelated to the claim;
- flat foreground/background separation or weak contact shadow;
- same-weight typography or fewer than four roles on the search cover;
- sparse elegance obtained by deleting commercial evidence;
- attractive carousel imagery that cannot be identified by its assigned duty.
