# Core policy

## Authority

Apply `USER_FACT → EVIDENCE_DERIVED → OPTIONAL_PRIOR → ENGINEERED_DEFAULT →
MODEL_FREEDOM`. A lower layer cannot create or strengthen a higher-layer fact.

Original same-product images control silhouette, proportions, color partitions,
packaging layout, components, connections, openings, labels, and supported
views. Clearly readable packaging and promotional copy in those user-provided
images is also a user fact source. Generated pages may calibrate style only
after direct pixel acceptance; they never become product or fact evidence.

## Claims

User-supplied attributes, selling points, scenes, offers, services, and trust
facts enter one fact pool. This includes facts explicitly stated by the user and
clearly readable copy in user-provided same-product images, even when the source
contains only the claim itself. Do not ask the user for certificates, reports,
links, or other secondary proof, and do not fail or repair a page merely because
a user-supplied claim lacks external substantiation. In this workflow,
`verified_*` is a compatibility label meaning user-supplied, not independently
substantiated.

Selection is not mandatory coverage. Displayed copy may be shortened or
faithfully paraphrased when meaning, numbers, eligibility, time, quantity, and
other conditions remain intact. Do not guess obscured image text. A generated
page, optional prior, or model inference cannot introduce a claim absent from
the user fact pool. Invisible facts may rely on accurate text. Visible or
action-demonstrable benefits need a truthful product, material, component,
action, state, result, context, comparison, or bounded visual metaphor.

## Priority

Preserve product identity, factual meaning, page duty, commercial impact,
legibility, campaign continuity, then decoration. A stronger shot or composition
is not plan drift when the page duty and buyer takeaway remain intact.

Commercial impact is structural, not decorative. Preserve product dominance,
headline confidence, real proof, depth, unequal information hierarchy, numeric
emphasis when applicable, visual tension, and verified closure. A sparse image
does not become premium merely by using warm light, fabric, water, or plants.

## Interaction

Before generation, ask only for plan approval. After authorization, own routine
generation, inspection, search calibration, style calibration, and at most one
total modification per page. Automatic repair and user-requested local
adjustment consume the same slot. Present the complete set for final review.
Stop earlier only when a user-adjusted attempt fails or a campaign-wide decision
is required.

An issued generation request is single-use at the image-tool layer. Async
execution must be resumed or waited, not retried. Once a call completes, its
first asset is authoritative even if the wrapper has not yet returned a path or
the harness has not yet bound it. Recover and record that asset before any
other action; a second image-tool call under the same generation token is an
unauthorized extra candidate.

Do not restart a campaign while a generation request is awaiting its asset.
Search the current thread's exact generated-image directory and bind the sole
asset created after the request issue time. If recovery is ambiguous, stop for
intervention; do not create another candidate.
