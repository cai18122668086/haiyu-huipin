# Six-page strategy

1. `SEARCH_MAIN_IMAGE_LISTING_COVER`: win a qualified list-page click with a
   dominant complete product, one strong verified hook, physical evidence, and
   commercial closure.
2. `CAROUSEL_IDENTITY_HERO`: establish product identity, material character,
   and campaign expression with a different shot and lower information density.
3. `CAROUSEL_CORE_FEATURE_PROOF`: show the most important mechanism, material,
   component, or functional relation.
4. `CAROUSEL_SCENE_OR_OPERATION`: establish credible human, hand, object, or
   spatial use relation.
5. `CAROUSEL_DETAIL_OR_TRUST`: resolve a decisive structural, material,
   interface, service, or trust question.
6. `CAROUSEL_OFFER_OR_SPEC_CLOSURE`: close with a verified offer when complete;
   otherwise use specification, compatibility, audience, service, or trust.

The search cover and carousel identity hero must differ in shot/crop, subject
anchor, reading path, copy topology, evidence topology, and commercial duty.
One page may combine related claims. Supporting overlap is acceptable when it
serves a different buyer question; adjacent pages must not repeat the same
bundle, proof object, and conclusion.

For the search cover, use two or three scan stops and a visible identity/header,
dominant product-message field, real proof or zero-to-five unequal information
modules, at least four type roles, and bottom closure. With a complete verified
offer, use one transaction anchor and at most two attached conditions. Without
one, preserve the commercial frame using verified non-price information. Do not
delete useful numeric, efficacy, specification, service, trust, or condition
information merely to create a sparse poster.

Use the compiled page-specific spatial archetypes, not page-number labels:

- search: asymmetric commercial frame with a short hook, dominant complete
  product, unequal modules or real action, and closure;
- identity: editorial diagonal or offset identity view, sparse copy, one real
  relationship, and no search-style bottom strip;
- proof: full-product plus real macro double scale;
- operation: diagonal contact/action path with copy bound to the action;
- detail: macro-dominant callout field with the full product secondary;
- closure: full product plus an unequal numeric/specification/offer cluster.

Adjacent pages fail when they reuse the same product side, copy side, dominant
view, evidence topology, and spatial skeleton even if their words differ.
